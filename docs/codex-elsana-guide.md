lifetime profession or a hobby. As a profession, the skill 
allows an adventurous non-combatant the ability to offer 
assistance to some hunting parties. To those who are less 
adventurous, locksmiths are a much-needed and highly-
appreciated commodity in the city of Iridine and its 
surrounding areas. To all those combatant folk, there is no 
need to explain why picking up the craft as a hobby would 
be to your benefit.
      Does clicking coffers all day sound like a bore to you? 
Or do you find yourself wanting more? There are many 
trainers, including the world-renowned and ever-busy 
Apula from Riverside Locks, who can teach you about 
anything you could want to know until you are outstanding! 
Speaking from personal experience, I find that the forging 
aspect of smith work is quite satisfying, not to mention, if 
you are forgetful like& someone I know& (not me at

      So think about it! Consider it! Laugh about it, cry about

paces north of the Bronze Lane marker point (the constable

bleed, OR&

an interest in becoming a recruit of ours, we can set you up 
with a nice silver lockpick, and usually we have nice 
coffers which are easier to unlock, and they are lockable, 
jammable, and unjammable as well. Those you can take 
anywhere, and make excellent fire wood when you get cold

Your interests as far as what you want to learn may vary

               Picking Coffers and Chests!
Coffers are generally easier to open than chests. The locks 
are simpler, as you will see when you look at them. Try 
these first. The best thing while starting out is to offer 
your services to any and all who will let you try on their 
collections, but be aware of your own skill level. If you 
find that a coffer or chest is too badly jammed, and you 
cant study it well enough to try with a greater chance of 
success, move on! Dont be ashamed, some are very tricky. 
If you jam them up too badly, a master smith may even 
have difficulty, and that leads to mean-faced clients. Im 
making a mean face to show you, can you see?? Yeah, you 
dont want that!
                    Steps!
Before You Start: Keep all of the coffers and chests on the 
ground or in a sack you are carrying. It can get confusing if 
you or your client tries to pick them up while you are 
working.
Step One: Unjam it! Remember, if its too hard, ignore it! 
Its motivation to keep perfecting your skill.
Step Two: Unlock it! This is easier, and there is no risk of 
jamming it up while you try. Keep trying!
Step Three: If the client doesnt care, collect the stubborn 
containers and bring them to me so that I can add them to 
my burn pile. It gets cold at the Dancing Cat during the 
rainy season.

---
                   Forging!
     Want to forge a key or lockpick?! Great!! Its my 
favorite. Heres how you do it.
                   Steps!
Step One: Get your tools! You need wax, clay, a crucible, 
tongs, and sometimes firewood. You can ask an outdoors 
person for help collecting this if you do, in fact, need it.
Step Two: Take the item you want to duplicate, and warm 
some wax around it. This may take several tries, because 
the wax can be stubborn. I promise, it gets very easy with 
lots of practice!
Step Three: Offer the item back to its owner, and then 
scoop out some clay. This particular clay is quite stubborn, 
so dont get it on your clothes if you can help it. It stains. 
THEN! Work the clay around the wax imprint you have 
created. This will create a nice little encased clay box of 
sorts. If you have a knife, you can label this mold should 
you or the client wish it.

---
Step Four: Grab your tongs! In the furnace room across 
from Apulas, light the second furnace, as this one creates a 
smaller flame. Heat the clay over that second furnace until 
it dries. This can get really hot, so I advise wearing gloves.
Step Five: Extinguish the second furnace and light the first
 one. Drop your tongs and the mold, making sure to keep it 
in plain sight of any clients present. Then! Grab your 
crucible and slag, and place the slag into the crucible. Pick 
your tongs back up and heat the crucible over the lit 
furnace. You will be able to tell when the metal is ready to 
use, because it will be a bright-orange liquid.
Step Six: Forge the tool with the crucible and mold you 
dropped. Make sure it is the only mold on the ground 
before forging it, just to be sure youre not making the 
wrong one.

---
Step Seven: Extinguish the furnace and pick up the mold. 
At this point you can either offer it to the owner, or 
crack it into dust. If you simply discard it, be aware 
that some scummy rat hunter may pick it up at the dumps! 
THIS CAN BECOME A PROBLEM. Just a warning.
     And thats it! Its really quite simple once you get 
the imprint and mold creation down to an art.
     Also note that if you forget any steps at any point, 
there is a beautifully-written sign in the furnace room!

            The Guild of Locksmiths
I mentioned briefly the Guild of Locksmiths in the first 
part of this book. We are a diverse crew of smiths who work 
tirelessly to improve our skill, and offer our services to 
the Iridine community. We have a special headquarters which 
members have access to, which has a private furnace room, 
tools, and at the time of me writing this, we hope to have a 
garden built sometime in the near future!
We charge residents a standard fee for our services, and in 
exchange, the grand-master smiths will train its members to 
an equal level. Other aspiring locksmiths who do not join 
are only entitled to free lessons to a practiced level by 
our members. Many locksmiths join for that reason, and then 
find they rather enjoy their place among us.
More information can be obtained by finding a GoL 
member. Were around most of the time, so all you have to 
do is think for us!

               Hunting as a Locksmith
Going on hunting trips with combatant residents can prove 
very profitable to a professional locksmith! Not to mention, 
it is fun if you like walking through DANGER, and it can 
even inspire you to learn new things! Like healing, to help 
the wounded warriors (and yourself)! Like hunting lore to 
collect high quality skins from fallen creatures! Like 
outdoor basics to build shelter for your exhausted 
comrades! The inspiration is ENDLESS!
Here are some tips if you decide to leave the city of 
Iridine in search of locksmith adventures!
-    Always travel in a group.
-    Have the person most capable of combat 
guarding protect you for the whole adventure. Stick 
close to this person so that you remain guarded! If you 
lose them, be sure to ask them not to forget they should 
be guarding you.
-    If you go to the Brigand Treehouse, the best 
places to stay are at the base of the tree (but dont 
move, or the archer might see you!) or in the center 

---
room, guarded by your trusty guard-man/woman-
person. You can offer to collect the chests and coffers 
from unconscious brigands and other equally filthy 
men, and unlock them as you get them. Keeping the 
sacks is good, too, so your companions or you (if 
youre very fast) can organize their acquired loot.
-    If you venture to the Ravines in Monlon, there is 
a room that usually has a mattress which is safe to stay 
in. If youre a very clean person, you might consider 
bringing a blanket along as the mattress is rather filthy. 
Also note that the hoards there use chests with more 
advanced lock mechanisms, so they are sometimes 
more difficult to get open.
-    Dont be afraid to charge them a little more for 
the danger and travel factors. Not to mention, you are 
saving them the hassle of bringing back the heavy 
chests, and you can all camp out for a while, thus 
increasing their hunting and loot-collecting efficiency! 
Just be sure to ask them ahead of time if your new fee is 
acceptable.
