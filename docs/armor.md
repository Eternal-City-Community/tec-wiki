# Armor

Armor in **The Eternal City** provides protection to specific body locations. Coverage, protection level, material, weight, and compatibility with other worn pieces all matter when comparing equipment.

## Armor Protection

Protection descriptions are shown in-game as qualitative values. When recording armor on the wiki, use the wording shown by the game rather than converting it into an assumed numeric value.

!!! note
    Armor data is being migrated and verified from the existing TEC wiki and player testing. Entries should preserve the exact in-game coverage and protection wording whenever possible.

## Armor Stacking

Exact stacking behavior should be documented from observed game behavior. Pieces that cover overlapping body locations may interact differently depending on armor type and equipment slot.

## Contributing Armor Data

When adding an armor entry, include the exact item name and, where known:

- Condition wording
- Covered body locations
- Protection wording
- Weight
- Material
- Any known stacking or wearing restrictions
