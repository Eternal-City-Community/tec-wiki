### Legend:
* **Short**: can only be used short range 
* **Long**: can only be used long range 
* **Either**: can be used either range 
* **Knockdown**: will change a character's posture from standing to: kneeling or laying 
* **Disarm**: will make the target unwield their weapon 
* **Pushback**: will make the target retreat 
* **Pull**: will pull the target towards you 
* **Parting**: will make you retreat 
* **Stepping**: will make you step up in stance (more offensive) 
* **Defensive**: will make you step down in stance (more defensive) 
* **Stun**: has a chance to stun your target 
* **AoE**: has a chance to strike all targets approached to you 
* **Draw**: skill that makes you quickly wield your weapon 
* **Grapple**: launches an attempt at grappling with a target (you initiate the attack, a second goes by and it then goes through, can be cancelled by opponent retreating or hitting you) 
* **Trap**: using your weapon, you attempt to trap your target/weapon 
* **Multi-hits**: has a chance to hit a target multiple times 
* **Bleed**: has a chance to create a bleeding wound 
* **Lunge**: will make you approach your target
