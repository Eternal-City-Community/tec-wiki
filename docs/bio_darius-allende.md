# Bio Darius Allende

>


**Darius Allende - High Priest's Proxy** 

He represents the political arm of the cult and serves them in the Senate. Generally thought of as an intelligent politician and reasonably pious man. With the two brothers running the cult, the political might of the Allende family seems assured. He is brother to [Tharius](/bio_tharius-allende/), the [High Priest](/cult-of-ereal/#High-Priest).
