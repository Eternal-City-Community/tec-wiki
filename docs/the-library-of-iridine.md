**Cataloguer's note:**
This is a non-exhaustive catalogue of the works that can be found in the City Library, with the aim to help any interrested reader to find his way around. As my research progresses, new versions of this catalogue will be posted, and different types of the catalogue, more or less detailed, will be available. If you are interrested in commissionning a specific kind of this catalogue, or having a more detailed version, please seek me out.

Happy reading to everyone!

#### Legend                                   
Location of shelves are presented in the following way:
**[direction from entry hall]**/**[room number]**/**[shelf number]**
For example, **W/2/1** would stand for the **1st shelf** in the **2nd room**, **west** from the entry hall.
