# The Eternal City Wiki

Welcome to the community-maintained wiki for **The Eternal City**, the long-running text-based MUD.

This replacement is being built to preserve the familiar look and feel of the TEC community wiki while improving search, reliability, mobile support, and long-term maintainability.

!!! note
    The replacement wiki is under active construction. The existing Wikidot wiki remains the primary reference while pages are migrated and reviewed.
