# Shields Guide

### Shields Guide
Shields are required for the [Shields](/shields/) skill set, as well as for certain other skillsets such as [Pardelian](/pardelian-one-handed-swords/) and [Hoplite Combat](/hoplite-combat/).

There are various factors that go into deciding which shield to use. The main ones are:
* **Shield Weight** - The weight of the shield.
* **Shield (offensive) Damage** - Damage dealt from shield attacks, often correlated to its weight.
* **Shield Absorption Rate** - How frequently the shield will absorb hits that make it past your blocks & dodges.
* **Shield HP** - How much damage the shield can absorb before breaking.

Any shield made of bronze or iron can be **[Re-Forged](/weapons/#Reforge)** by **Ferrarius**.

To understand how much damage your shield has taken, you can INSPECT it. It will go from, "It has sustained a **light** amount of damage", "It has sustained a **moderate** amount of damage", "It has sustained a **significant** amount of damage" to then being destroyed. Repair your shield before it is destroyed, as extra costs are incurred to repay a destroyed shield.
