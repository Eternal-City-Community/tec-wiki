# Avros Guide

## Avros Gladius Combat Guide

Below you'll find everything important to know about the Avros Gladius style.

### In a Nutshell
#### Pros
* You can also use Skill Points **(SP) from [Swords](/one-handed-swords/)** to learn Avros.
* [Stab and Slash](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is both a **double-hitter** & **defense-penetrating** attack.
* [Whirling Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is a **hard-hitting maneuver**, capable of [5th level damage](/character-condition/#Wounds).
* [Forced Thrust](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is a **defense-penetrating** attack, very effective against polearms.
* [Needle Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is a **quadruple-hitting attack**.
* [Strike and Smash](/avros-one-handed-swords/#Strike) has a chance to **knock down your target**.
* [Flinging Disarm](/avros-one-handed-swords/) can **free your trapped weapon** from an axe, cestus or trident.
* When [Flailing Defense](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is performed successfully, it will **negate the benefits of the target's [Perception](/stats/#Perception)** for their next attack.
* [Sunrise Block](/avros-one-handed-swords/#Sunrise) is an **additional sword block** that can override weaker [swords blocks](/one-handed-swords/) that cover the same defensive area.


#### Cons
* [Rapid Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) has a **chance to drop** your weapon on a missed roll. This will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Avros Gladius Combat (Basics)](/avros-one-handed-swords/).
* [Whirling Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) has a **chance to leave openings** on a missed roll. This will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Avros Gladius Combat (Basics)](/avros-one-handed-swords/).
* [Forced Thrust](/avros-one-handed-swords/) will **always leave openings** on a missed roll and can only be used on a target wielding a weapon. The openings will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Avros Gladius Combat (Basics)](/avros-one-handed-swords/).
* [Strike and Smash](/avros-one-handed-swords/#Strike) will **always leave openings** on a missed roll and can only be used on a standing target wielding a weapon. The openings will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Avros Gladius Combat (Basics)](/avros-one-handed-swords/).
* Using Avros means the investment in a completely separate skill set, outside of Swords and Shields. This will likely lead to **longer training and more overall SP** required to progress to higher tiers of combat.
* While **[Needle Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/)** hits 4 times, overall it's **relatively weak** and a more damaging triple-hitting maneuver can outperform it.


#### Additional Notes
* [Pivot Lunge](/avros-one-handed-swords/) is a **partial-disengage** in the sense that it will **keep you approached to your target**, but **retreat you from anyone else** otherwise approached to you. 
* If [Sunrise Block](/avros-one-handed-swords/#Sunrise) overlaps with another [sword block](/one-handed-swords/), the **block with the higher [Rank Bonus](/rank-bonus-calculator/)** is taken. See the [Blocks & Dodges](/blocks-and-dodges/) spreadsheet for an idea of where there may be an overlap.


### Advice & Tips
* All attacks that have chances to make the **wielder fumble, leave openings, fall down, etc.** will **no longer do so** after **achieving rank 90** in the relevant parent skill. This is **highly recommended**.

### Weapons
#### Summary
In most cases, if still using Reflexes, you'll want a **retalq gladius**. If your character has maxed their Speed stat at Outstanding, a **reforged boison gladius** may prove more beneficial.

For detailed information, **see the [Sword Guide](/one-handed-swords-guide/)**.

### Moves
#### Worthwhile
* [Stab and Slash](/avros-one-handed-swords/) - A strong, double-hitting attack with defense-penetrating capabilities and a high chance to crit.
* [Whirling Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) - A heavy-hitting attack, aimed high by default.
* [Forced Thrust](/avros-one-handed-swords/) - A good attack against polearms, but can only be used against standing opponents wielding a weapon.
* [Rapid Strike](/avros-one-handed-swords/) - A good chopping attack.


#### Less useful
* [Pivot Lunge](/avros-one-handed-swords/) - Parting-type attack, retreating you from everyone *except* your target. Hard to land and your other opponents can typically reapproach easily.

### Complementary Skill Sets
* [Shields](/shields/) is near-mandatory.
* [Nelsor Gladius Combat](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/) is highly encouraged.
* [Brawling](/brawling/) is highly encouraged.
