We Dance this day the one last time
Blood flows it won't be long
We dance this dance one last time
Erai'Theran please hear our song 
We sing the song our fathers sung
We smile as we face the end
Never letting our guard down
To our fate you will thus send
To those we loved send happy words
for we die but not in vain
To our enemies send heave hearts
The Altenes rally again
For hear I shall rest unto the end
And Fight I will no more
The final dance was danced with me
And peace be mine ever more.

Toss the dice
    Where ever they lie
Toss the dice
    Worst case we die
Toss the dice
    Whatever the cost
Toss the dice
    For what not risked is lost
Toss the dice
    However they fall
Toss the Dice
    When love is involved
Toss the dice
    Little pips on clay  
Toss the dice 
    Nothing left to say 

---
Toss the dice
    Till the little girl cries
Toss the dice
    Till the sun does rise
Toss the Dice
    You never had a choice
Toss the dice
    It is your only voice
Toss the dice
    While you draw breath
Toss the dice
    When they stop you meet death

Oh I dont know if you understand
Whats going through my head
It all slowly fades away
As I sink into my bed
Yet as my eyelids start to fall
In to the void my mind will flee
I know I wont find any rest
For in dreams you visit me 
Chaos running over me
It tears my soul apart
Yet your memory pulls me back
I got a little sunshine in my heart

---
We'll dance all night till theres no pain
And sing our song till our voices strain
All just to prove to us were sane
Then we will dance the dance of Glory
The girls that blush are truly shy
But love for us will make them cry
So in Love for them we will wave goodbye
For we will dance the dance of Glory
 Days come on the sun hangs low
And if it was all chance we'll never know
But the pain will come and it will go
When we will dance the dance of glory
 Some we Love will let us down
If fools have a king well wear a crown
But if they look back they will see a smile and not a frown
Oh come our dance of glory

Long arms Embrace
Threw pain, anger, and strife
Morning Glories bloom
After darkness there is life
As sunlight flows
Through this lifes window pane
Darkness retreats
Light erasing darkness stain
Son of morning
All father of the guiding light
Blazing rays
Pierce the gloom with holy might
Accept this child
Let never your guard Yield
Flowers grow 
Ever more on this potter's field
Long arms Embrace
Through pain, anger, and strife
Morning Glories bloom
Proof after darkness there is life

---
Beauty blooming when darkness falls
Yet hiding in the light
All the day it disappears
Hiding in plain sight
Why does this lovely flower hide?
When people are around
Seeing itself as a weed
Believing itself bound
Oh if only this little flower
Saw itself in my own eyes
It would know its blossoms beauty
It would see its lies 
It would see its self-won glory
It would see the light
But sadly it hides bathed in darkness
Beloved by the night

Now something silly and foolish
I see you there watching me
    You hairy homely git
I steam mutter and bluster
    But watching me you sit
Munching on the soft sweet fruit
    You chatter happily
All I see is I'll have no pie
    And you still laugh at me
Once I came running out
    But I was made the fool
Quick as light you went up the tree
    Chattering so cruel
Oh some day you will be slow
    Or maybe I'll get a cat
And then I will stride proud threw town
    In my new squirrel skin hat
