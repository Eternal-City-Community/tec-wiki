# Basran Fount

Adorning the very top of Basran Hill is the famed Basran Fount, a monument set around a natural spring. Installed by [bio:Tulca I](/bio_tulca-i/), first King of Iridine in the center of the Old City, the Basran Fount is decorated by reliefs showing Tulcas victories over the scattered tribes of what is now Iridine. The fount is kept in good repair and visited regularly by travelers and pigeons alike.
