# Hg Shipwreck

## Coastal Shipwreck

This area is described as a **lower-mid** to **mid tier** hunting ground.


**Suggested: [@mtarg](/macros-and-targeting/) seafarer|sailor|marine|gull**


#### Highlights
* Careful! You can slip and fall if climbing in from the north-west (QH Beach), seriously injuring yourself.
* A mix of humanoids *(sailors, seafarers)* & creatures *(gulls)*.
* Chance of **marines** spawning on the ship.
* A functional **forge** for [locksmithing](/locksmithing/).
* Humanoids in this area **do not carry sacks**, bring your own.


### Directions
Go to the cliff where you climb down into the Sea Caves area (Not the sewer entrance, the OTHER one). If you go east, thats the old Sea Caves. Go northwest for the new ones.

**Guide**
* Wrecked ship:
 * Use the **swim <direction>** command to traverse flooded (water) tiles. You'll need to swim down into the flooded shoal depths and then back upward into the wrecked ship to board it, and you'll need to do the reverse to get back to the shoreline.
 * To get loot off the ship, carry sacks to location **A** in the flooded shoal depths and drop them. They will float upward to location **A** near the coastline's rocky outcrop. You can then swim upward, grab the sacks, and toss them northeast.
* Underground hangout:
 * This is a raid-style hunting area where you must defeat a large number of enemies to proceed to the next room. Best done with a group of fighters teaming up together.


It will keep going in a western and northerly direction. Its fairly linear in layout so you can't really get lost.

To get onto the ship, there will be a section of the cliffwalk path that has 3 tiny squares running southeast to northwest. In the middle square, you can go southwest into a BIG square. Entering this big square will put you into water, similar to clam diving. From that big square, swim down, then southwest again, then up, then west to get to the deck of the shipwreck.

If you carry loot out and it is heavy, you will sink. The trick to this is, Remove the sacks of loot and carry them in your hand. When you swim out, swim south, northeast and then drop the sacks. When you swim Up from this room, your loot sacks will float up after a few seconds. Grab the sacks, toss them northeast (to the middle of the 3 tiny squares mentioned earlier) , then swim northeast and you will be back on land with your loot at your feet.
