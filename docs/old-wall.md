# Old Wall

Constructed around the Old City on Basran Hill, the Old Wall surrounds what is said to be the first city of Iridine, founded by Tulcas.
Very little of the actual wall itself still stands; most of the remaining sections stand on the northwest and southwest sides of the hill. 
The parts that do stand, however, serve as a testament to the spirit of the early Iridinians that built the foundations of the modern city.
