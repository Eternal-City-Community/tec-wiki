# Cullaiden Island Temple

### Abandoned Temple

No one seems to know the origins of this temple which is long past its prime. Some areas of the temple are falling to ruin, but it still remains standing on its solid foundation in the middle of the swamp area which is located pretty much in the center of Cullaiden Island. A warm breeze comes from the depths of the underground tunnel system and can be felt throughout the temple. Solitary wasps and glossy black wasps have made the underground tunnel system their home.

Sharina, a dedicated member of the Cult of Ereal is taking care of a small coma ward and will tend to your wounds if you are in need. She can be found upstairs.
