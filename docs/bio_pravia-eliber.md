# Bio Pravia Eliber

>


## Revealer Pravia Eliber
The leader of the Temple of Illumination was one of countless orphans roaming the streets of Iridine. Like those she grew up with, she spent her days eking out an existence in the steps. When she was still quite young, a priest in the Temple of Illumination noticed her, and perceived an aptitude. She was adopted into the Temple as an Eliber child, one raised by Ereal. She has quickly risen through the ranks in the Sect of Revealing Light, and is in charge of her own temple at the young age of 35. This was partially due, no doubt, to her desire to work in the little sought after Steps Temple. She is known for her desire to help the street children and her knowledge and understanding of how the Steps work. Some whisper that she has close friends in several gangs, but there is no evidence of this.
