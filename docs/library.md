# Library

## Iridine Library

The Library of Iridine is located within the [Forum in Iridine](/forum/). To get there, type "walk to library" from a main road. 

**Weapons** are not allowed in the Library. You must ensure you have **no weapons** on you or give them to the tan-clad guard outside the library before entering. The guard will hold *(multiple)* weapons for you, and return them if you tell him to "return" them.

Be on your best behavior inside or you might find yourself banned permanently.


### Languages
The Library of Iridine has many well-spoken tutors within its halls, for those wishing to broaden their horizons. For more information, see **[learning languages](/languages/)** *(including related commands)*.


### Finding Literature

Unrelated to [language lessons](/languages/), there are many rooms and sections in the library, on a wide range of topics. You can **search** a shelf for **a specific topic**, grab a **random title** or look at **all titles on a shelf**.

* **Search for a specific topic**: type "search shelf for <KEYWORD>"
* **Get a random title**: type "search shelf"
* **Review all titles**: type "search shelf for ." (be sure to include the period)

Be sure to explore all the rooms, as each one has a unique selection of reading materials.

You **cannot take materials out** of the library. When you are finished with your reading, simply **leave it on the ground** for re-shelving.

For a **list of *(some)* content** currently or previously submitted, **consult the [library archive](/library-archive/)**.

### Contributing to the Library
If you have written a **piece of fiction**, **scholarly work** or **historical record**, you can submit it for consideration for the library shelves. **Put a copy in the large oak box outside the Library** for consideration and review by a member of the TEC staff.
