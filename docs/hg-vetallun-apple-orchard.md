# Hg Vetallun Apple Orchard

[Hunting Grounds](/hunting-grounds/) » Vetallun Apple Orchard

### Overview
Situated just off the heart of the military town of Vetallun, this seclusive apple orchard offers peace and quiet for those who wish to take a breather. Come and have a taste of Galbria's apples; clean the ground off of rotten apples, or even eliminate those pesky wasps and worms that bother the apple harvesters.

#### Creatures
* a fruit wasp
* a long pale worm
* a cocoon (WARNING: will spawn a very dangerous large wasp once destroyed that requires high skill to defeat)
