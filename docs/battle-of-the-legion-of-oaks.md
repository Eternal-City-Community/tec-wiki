# Battle Of The Legion Of Oaks

15 YP. [Valstaron Martius](/bio_valstaron-martius/) cemented the victory over Aestiva at the Battle of the Legion of Oaks. He was struck by an Aestivan spear at that battle, and several months later died from the lingering effects of the wound. He was declared a Hero of the Republic and given a tribute postumously, during which his body was paraded through the streets in the traditional garland.
