# Hoplite Combat Guide

Below you'll find everything important to know about Hoplite Combat.

### In a Nutshell
#### Pros
* **Six double-hitting attacks.** These include the only Easy-difficulty multi-hitters across all skillsets.
* **Bonus to shield bashes.** Hoplite Combat Stance gives a small bonus to [shield bashes](/shields/).
* **Stepping Maneuvers.** Includes both offensive ([Rising Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#rthrust)) & defensive ([Defensive Repel](/hoplite-combat/#drepel)) stepping maneuvers.
* **Unique shield/spear combinations.** You can choose to mix and match different spears and shields, to get a wide variety of offensive and defensive combinations. Both the chosen shield and the chosen spear impact the speed and strength of your attacks.

#### Cons
* **Close range combat.** While having a number of either-range maneuvers, Hoplite Combat is best-suited for close range combat.
* **Hard to combine with Spears skillset.** While possible to use some Spears skillset moves with Hoplite, you lose stance bonus for Spear maneuvers and cannot access many of the more powerful [Spears](/spears/) attacks with one hand only.
* **Requires Shields.** Requires a secondary skillset ([Shields](/shields/)) for almost all defense. Hoplite Combat has only one block which is used to defend against rare traps and similar maneuvers, it does not block typical attacks.

#### Additional Notes
* You can use Skill Points (SP) from [Spears](/spears/) to learn Hoplite Combat.
* Both traditional (long) spears and short spears can be used as weapons for Hoplite Combat. Use the <code>1wield</code> command to wield a traditional long spear in one hand for use with Hoplite combat, or the <code>dualwield</code> command if you are holding a shield and spear in each hand.


### Advice & Tips
* Achieving **rank 100 in [Hoplite Combat Stance](/hoplite-combat/#hoplite)** early on is highly recommended. It gives bonuses to attacks and will auto-assume at high ranks.
* When wielding a [traditional long spear](/spears/), you can also perform **[Spear Jab](/spears/#Jab)**, **[Spear Stab](/spears/#Stab)**, **[Spear Overhead Thrust](/spears/#Thrust)**, **[Spear Parting Jab](/spears/#Pjab)**, **[Spear Throw](/spears/#Throw)** & **[Spear Feint](/spears/#Feint)** while fighting in the Hoplite Combat stance, though they receive no stance bonus from Hoplite Stance. 
* While both [Hoplite Feint](/hoplite-combat/#hfeint) (<code>hfeint</code>) & [Spear Feint](/spears/#Feint) (<code>feint</code>) are available, **[Hoplite Feint](/hoplite-combat/#hfeint)** is the preferred choice, as it auto-aims high and benefits from [Hoplite Combat Stance](/hoplite-combat/#hoplite).

#### Summary

Spear:
* Boison spears do the most damage, especially when reforged.
* Retalq does very good damage, is faster than boison, and has a higher critical hit rate.

Shield:
* Triangular shield does the most damage, but will not passively absorb any hits and is heavy.
* Towering shield with metal reinforcements does the second most damage, weighs two pounds less, and will absorb some hits.
* Bucklers or round shields are a lightweight option, but do significantly less damage.
* Wall shield or debris shield are the best options for defensive absorption but also do significantly less damage.

### Moves
#### Best Options
* [Whirling Strike](/hoplite-combat/#wstrike) - A highly-bonused double-hitting attack. Must be trained to high levels to increase the chance of a double hit.
* [Leaping Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#lthrust) - A highly-bonused attack that aims high by default.
* [Bash and Jab](/hoplite-combat/#bjab) - A highly-bonused easy-difficulty double-hitting attack with good damage, though it is slow.
* [Sweep and Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#swthrust) - A combined sweep & strike maneuver with a small bonus to hit. Must be trained to high levels to achieve a high chance of knockdown.
* [Lunge and Strike](/hoplite-combat/#lstrike) - A highly-bonused impossible-difficulty double-hitting attack. High damage and high crit-rate. Must be trained to high levels to increase the chance of a double hit.
* [Phalanx Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#pthrust) - Easy maneuver with favorable block matchups against some weapons.


#### Worthwhile
* [Shielded Stab](/hoplite-combat/#shstab) - Easy maneuver that always hits twice, but is fairly simple to dodge or block.
* [Slam and Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#sthrust) - Difficult maneuver that always hits twice and does very good damage, but has a penalty to hit.
* [Shielded Advance](/hoplite-combat/#sadvance) - A good utility maneuver that engages the target and automatically disengages a random enemy if you were already fully surrounded.
* [Rising Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#rthrust) - A stepping maneuver that enters a more aggressive stance as part of the attack.
* [Defensive Repel](/hoplite-combat/#drepel) - A stepping maneuver that enters a more defensive stance as part of the attack, and also retreats from the target.


#### Less useful
* [Underhand Thrust](/hoplite-combat/#uthrust) - An average attack with no special bonuses, and which is fairly easy for most weapons to block.
* [Rotating Bash](/hoplite-combat/#rbash) - An average attack with no special bonuses, and which is fairly easy for most weapons to block.
* [Stab and Swing](/hoplite-combat/#sswing) - Average attack that has a penalty to hit.
* [Spinning Chop](/hoplite-combat/#schop) - Difficult attack that has a small penalty to hit.
