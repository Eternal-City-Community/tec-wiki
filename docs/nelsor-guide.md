# Nelsor Guide

## Nelsor Gladius Combat Guide

Below you'll find everything important to know about the Nelsor gladius style.

### In a Nutshell
#### Pros
* You can use Skill Points **(SP) from [Swords](/one-handed-swords/)** to train Nelsor *(and vice versa)*.
* **[Reverse Sky Arch](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#RSA)** has defense penetrating capabilities, is a difficult attack to block and inflicts good damage.
* **[Extended Arm Spin](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#EAS)** is a **default-aimed high knockdown maneuver**, capable of unwielding your opponent's weapon if blocked.
* **[Kicking Slash](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#KS)**, and **[Lightning Thrust](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#LT)** are also very worthwhile to train.
* **[Kicking Leap Strike](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#KLS)** allows you to approach a target, even while already approached.
* **[Tiger Slash](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#TS)** is a strong-hitting double-hitter, default-aimed low.
* **[Vulture Block](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#VB)** is can be performed from a **prone (kneeling) position** 1-handed, or from a standing position if wielding 2-handed.

#### Cons
* This is not a standalone skill set and many prerequisites require you to **first learn [One-Handed Swords](/one-handed-swords/)**.
* Using Nelsor means the investment in a completely separate skill set, outside of Swords and Shields. This will likely lead to **longer training and more overall SP** required to progress to higher tiers of combat.
* The following maneuvers **leave defensive openings** on a (1-handed) failed attempt: [Reverse Sky Arch](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#RSA), [Kicking Slash](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#KS) & [Kicking Leap Strike](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#KLS). Wielding your gladius **2-handed** will prevent these openings. These openings will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Nelsor Gladius Combat (Basics)](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/).
* **[Spinning Duck and Strike](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#SDaS) may leave you kneeling** after an attempt. You can reduce this chance with ranks in [Simple Rolling Rise](/combat-maneuvers/#Rise) or [Backwards Rolling Rise](/combat-maneuvers/#BRise). Remaining in a kneeling position will **no longer occur** one you've reached grandmaster (90 ranks) in [Nelsor Gladius Combat (Basics)](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/).


### Advice & Tips
* Ranks in [Simple Rolling Rise](/combat-maneuvers/#Rise) or [Backwards Rolling Rise](/combat-maneuvers/#BRise) can provide a significant chance to rising from a [Spinning Duck and Strike](#SDaS).
* All attacks that have chances to make the **wielder fumble, leave openings, fall down, etc.** will **no longer do so** after **achieving rank 90** in [Nelsor Gladius Combat (Basics)](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/).
* Wielding your gladius 2-handed will allow you to perform [Vulture Block](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#VB) while standing.

### Weapons
In most cases, if still using Reflexes, you'll want a **retalq gladius**. If your character has maxed their Speed stat at Outstanding, a **reforged boison gladius** may prove more beneficial.

For detailed information, **see the [Sword Guide (Weapons)](/one-handed-swords-guide/#Weapons)**.

### Moves
#### Worthwhile
* [Reverse Sky Arch](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#RSA) - A **strong, reliable attack** for most opponents.
* [Kicking Slash](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#KS) - A solid attack **effective against polearms**.
* [Extended Arm Spin](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#EAS) - A versatile attack, defaults aiming high with a chance to knock your opponent down.
* [Lighting Thrust](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#LT) - A good, distanced attack.

#### Less useful
* [Planting the Blade](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#PtB) - Just for show, does nothing.
* [Arch of the Sky](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/#AotS) - Powerful, but slow to execute and hard to land.
