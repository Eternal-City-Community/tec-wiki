# Gardens Of Sunset

Along the road running east from Forum Axonus, past Forum Mico a good ways, is a large garden north of the road.  There are five entrances that lead to the wilderness that was once obviously a formal and well-tended city garden.  Trails lead to the remains of two large columned expanses with broken and worn ornaments of another time.  At the center is a spiral that seems to be tended more carefully.  For those that leave the overgrown trails, ancient trees choked by brambles with a smattering of small clearings can be found.  Some claim that broken statues are scattered through the garden, choked by creepers and broken by time.  The saying 'You may have to take a plunge into Sunset' is an allusion to the dangers of wandering off the paths, and the need that drives some to do so in difficult circumstances.  Often, a person could hide in the overgrowth and evade pursuit; however, it is rumored that dangers lurk in the wilderness, too.  Some of the more foolhardy that have wandered from the path speak of desperate men and women hiding in the shadows waiting to ambush the unwary.  Perhaps they merely saw a tree's shadow cast in the fading light of sunset.
