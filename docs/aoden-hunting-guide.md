# Aoden Hunting Guide

Hunting Grounds Version 1 by Aoden:

#### Preface {#Preface}
 
This volume is intended as a complete technical price guide of the various bestial hunting grounds available to an Iridinian hunter. This guide has been compiled with flawless cuts sold by a respected and skilled broker at the most generous carcass buyer. With those factors in mind, personal experience may vary. Of specific note, the heads of all beasts require careful handling and are worth significantly less when skinned by any other than a true master. Also of note, many animals manage to collect precious stones in their stomachs. These will not be noted in the listings. Rats, spiders, hounds, and other small vermin are the 
common suspects.
 
This volume will be structured so that each page will detail a hunting grounds. In cases where different areas of the same locale provide different experiences, additional pages will detail the additional areas. Each page will provide a brief description of the area, detailed directions for reaching the area, subjective rankings for the difficulty and profitability of the area, and a complete chart of animals and their skinnable parts. Each part will have the count, weight, and price of an ideal cut detailed. Each animal will have a price 
and weight total for what I, personally, think is worth skinning. A star will be placed next to each part included in those totals. Personal preference may vary.
 
The difficulty and profitability of each area is entirely subjective and based on various factors. The difficulty will include danger; an area with leech spiders that perpetually drown you in web will be ranked higher, despite those critters being killed by a few light taps of an inexperienced hunter. Profitability takes into account critter density, proximity to good part buyers, and the amount of effort required. A good hunter will take those rankings, and this guide as a whole, as a reference and determine what they are most comfortable with.
 
Aoden Muisech

#### Signal Tower Island {#STI}
 
Commonly referred to as "the Island", this area is a good choice for the first 
area any new fighter should visit. Small and weak vermin wander the large island 
and rarely swarm. A skilled combatant will kill these vermin very quickly.

north, three paces west, and then onto the northwest ramp. Once there, wait for 
and board a ferry that continuously shuttles back and forth to the island. Adult 
fluviturs can be found on the ship grounded on the northwest side.

#### Riverside Sewers - Entrance {#Sewers1}
 
The sewers can be divided into three areas, which are roamed by different 
beasts. The entrance of the sewers is the natural progression from the Island, 
with small vermin for an unskilled combatant to fight. The southern branch is 
of similar difficulty. The southeastern branch, however, has large wasps, 
alligators, and spiders that are more dangerous.
 
The sewers can be entered by starting outside the Stone Toga, stepping a pace 
east, entering the northern door, stepping another pace east, and then climbing 
the ladder all the way down. 
 
Note: Entering the sewers will leave you covered in filth and smelling horribly. 
There is a barrel of clean water and a brush above the ladder. You can scrub 
yourself down with the brush to remove the odor.

#### Iridine City Dumps {#Dumps}
 
The city dumps, located just outside the walls, is the final home of much of 
the city’s solid refuse. All of this trash understandably attracts swarms of
well-fed rats and wild dogs. This area is slightly more difficult than the 
entrance of the sewers.
 
Note: Skilled combatants entering the dumps will be chased off by crazy old
trash-dwellers. They are quite dangerous, allowing the dumps to be enjoyed by
younger fighters.
 
To reach the dumps, walk to the Junk Dealer, step west a pace, step northwest a 
pace, and then enter the dumps to the north.

#### Under the Boardwalk {#Boardwalk}
 
The southwest edge of Quartz Heights is adorned with a boardwalk elevated off
of the beachfront. While the boardwalk is home to colorful shops and delicious 
food, the beach it is built over swarms with large sandcrabs. The area might be
found useful to inexperienced hunters that are looking for either a fairly calm
experience or a sack full of crab meat.
 
Walk to Emerald Square. From there, follow the broad white cobblestone road to
the west. Turn southwest after a few paces and continue to follow the white 
cobblestone road. Follow it to the end, turning west along it when it curves. 
You will have stopped on a wide gray flagstone thoroughfare, just before a narrow 
alley between large, crowded insulae. Take two paces southeast and one south onto 
a sandstone thoroughfare. Follow it directly west to its end. Follow the rough
cobblestone road southward until a boardwalk barker loudly announces your 
arrival. Mount the boardwalk and follow it west until you reach an intersection.
The trapdoor leading down to the beach is to your south.
 
Difficulty: 1/5
Profit:     1/5

#### Old City - Moondeep {#OldCity}
 
Moondeep is an ancient burial ground in the Old City, which is rumored to be
the original center of Iridine. Now that it has fallen to ill-repair, snakes,
carrion beetles, and large acid-spitting lizards have found a home. The lizards
are quite dangerous and some training is needed to hunt here.
 
Walk to the Colosseum, step east until you reach the crossroads, step east 
down an ancient cobbled road, follow that north until you reach an intersection,
step west three paces, and then the area to your direct south is where the 
beasts roam.
 
Note: The fire-lizards very accurately spit acid that can eat into clothing and 
armor.

#### Harbor Sea Caves {#SeaCaves}
 
The harbor sea caves are a natural set of caverns carved into the sea cliffs by
an ancient underground river. The river has since disappeared, but the caverns
have been broken into by some industrious sewer beast and are now accessible
from the Riverside sewers. Serpents, massive crabs, and lost sailors wander 
around the caverns.
 
Follow the directions required to reach the southern branch of the Riverside
sewers. Follow the path until an intersection. Take the northwestern path until
you reach a rusty grate. At the bottom of the grate is the entrance to the sea
caves.
 
Note: The western end of the sea caves exits to the sea, at the bottom of a
sheer cliff. A talented climber can use this as a less odorous shortcut to the
caverns. Bring plenty of sacks or a wagon, as the profitable crab parts are
very bulky.

maintenance leads to regular blockages that cause flooding and provides ample
residence for vermin and the large, dangerous reptiles that feed on them.
 
Walk to the Hospice of the Morning Light. From there, walk east until you pass
under the portcullis that marks entry into the Steps. From there, step east two
paces and take the dusty lane northward. Follow that until you can take a 
similarly wide lane eastward. Follow that to the end and stop before entering
any building. Step north into the alley, and west through the door. The first
entrance to the sewers is downward from here.
 
Note: There are dozens of entrances into the sewers. The above is the closest 
to the portcullis. The nature of the Steps sewers leaves many passages blocked 
by sewage and other entrances may be preferable for hunting.

#### Franlius Sewers {#FSewers}
 
The Franlius sewers weave under the war-torn streets. The usual excreta is the 
least of an adventurer’s concerns, as the Cinerans use the locale as a prison.
Debilitatingly venomous sail snakes glide through tunnels ridden with crippling 
traps.
 
From the town square on our side of the river, follow the road to the north 
until you reach the town square on the Cineran side. Follow the road east from
there. Step north just before the end of the road and then east into the stables. 
The entrance to the sewers is just south of you.
 
Note: Do not go here unless you are immensely confident in your ability to 
dodge. A single bite from a sail snake, if it pierces your thigh armor, will
slowly inflame until you are unable to move that leg. Make sure to enter the
area warily, only focusing more on your offense after you are confident in 
your defense. Also beware the crippling traps and the entrance trapdoor
being locked by Cinerans. It’s essential to bring a locksmith, and highly 
suggested to learn your way from someone knowledgeable of the layout.
 
Difficulty: 5/5
Profit:     4/5
