# Chainblade

### Skill Overview

Chainblades are a mobile, fast-attacking weapon consisting of twin blades connected by a chain. They allow the wielder to slash enemies up close or attack from a distance. The Chainblade is a weapon first introduce by [The Kelestians](/kelestia/).

**For guidance on using the skill set, see the [Chainblade Guide](/chainblade-guide/)**.

### Chainblade No Mind Strike  *nomind <target>* {#nomind}

Clearing your mind, you allow your body to instinctively decide on the next attack to perform. 

* This maneuver will **strike using your *best attack*** against an opponent, taking into account repeat penalties & high/mid/low targeting.
* This maneuver **receives a to-hit bonus** to the selected attack. 
 * Rank 1 = +5 to-hit bonus. 
 * Rank 2 = +6 to-hit bonus. 
 * Every subsequent 10 ranks increases the bonus by +1 until a cap of **rank 100 = +15 to-hit bonus**.
* This skill has a **30-second cooldown** after it's been used. While in cooldown, if you attempt to use the skill again too soon, you will receive the message. *"You can't seem to calm your mind right now."*

**When you see this in use you see:**

<div class="skill-template">

Your surroundings blur and fade as you clear your mind and flow effortlessly into your next strike.
[Success: 5, Roll: 63] You swing your retalq chainblade in a slow arc and pull, slashing across a dashing Kelestian warrior with the blade!  Critical Hit! Your eyes widen and a dashing Kelestian warrior's narrow as your swing barely catches him.

</div>
