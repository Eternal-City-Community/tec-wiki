# Bio Nathanial

>


## Nathanial Raius

Nathanial is the current head of the [Shrikes](/shrikes/). Now 32 years old, he joined the Shrikes as a small child whilst smuggling items into the city under his shirt for rich merchants and patricians. Since that time, he has slowly worked his way up the gang's ladder to his current position at its head. While he is quite skilled at most of the gang’s endeavors, Nathanial's gifts in the area of diplomacy and subterfuge are definitely lacking. It remains to be seen whether or not he can keep control of his office.
