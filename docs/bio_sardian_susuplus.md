# Sardian Susuplus

## Nationality

Iridine

## Appearance

You see a tall muscular man. He has steel eyes set above a distinguished chin in a severe tan face.
He is wearing some ruby colored boots studded with bronze, a bronze helm, accented with pieces of ruby, a phylax, a lorica hamata, some mail shoulder guards, a large sack, a faded cloth sack, a leather belt with iron studs, a studded black waistguard bearing a large forest-green 'TM' insignia, some stiff leather pteryges, a bronze greave, a bronze greave, a thin iron ring with a single small, well-cut piece of ruby fitted into it, some leather gloves with bronze studs, a black fine linen tunic edged with a row of green leaves, and a vivid red linen cloak.

## Background & History

Born and raised in Iridine with his brother Tondrin Susuplus.

Tondrin was viscously killed by bandits in the Traevant woods. This set Sardian on a life long quest of helping people traverse the Traevant woods and near by roads.

During this time he ran into a man named Kered Solstice, who would become a life long friend. With the help of a few others, they created the Traevant Militia, a group dedicated to the protection of travelers that considers each other as Family.

You'll know who is part of the Traevant Militia family by their wasitguards with a prominent "TM" the words 'Traevant Militia' in large fancy lettering. On both sides of the hip area are similar small plaques, enscribed into them both are the words 'Family For Life'.
