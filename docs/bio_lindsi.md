# Bio Lindsi

>


My name is Lindsi Freeman. I came to Iridine when I was 18 years old. I've learned a lot of new and wonderful things since I've arrive after having a huge quarrel with my ex-sister [bio:Avril](/bio_avril/). Almost as soon as I settled down, I met a wonderful guy, [bio:Antequides](/bio_antequides/), whom I am going to marry soon. When I first came, I just walked around a lot, but now I have learned how to make money and have fun. I like to help people out and despite what some people think of me, I manage to ignore them.
