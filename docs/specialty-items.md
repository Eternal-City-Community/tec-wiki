# Specialty Items

### Reforging (Ferrarius) {#Reforge}
***Ferrarius** is located in the [Town of Vetallun](/town-of-vetallun/) and for the cost of 1 voucher from [Fama](/shops/) will reforge a weapon made of metal, **excluding retalq** (i.e. tin, bronze, iron or boison). Reforging will permanently add a quality and/or damage boost to the weapon being reforged, and can only be done once to a weapon. With weapon quality word levels spanning a range, reforging an existing superior quality weapon will still result in a more beneficial weapon than an excellent quality weapon which results in superior after the process.

**This process will add the following detail to the long description of the item:**

> A small, stylized letter 'F' has been struck into the base of the metal.
