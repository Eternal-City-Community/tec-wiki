# Hg Bandit Forest

The Bandit Forest is home to a group of men and women intent on stealing from travelers and caravans that head north towards Vetallun and the port city of Blackvine. They have been deemed beyond redemption and are no longer contributors to society and can be killed and looted by anyone. The bandits here can quickly swarm any traveler on the road or in the forest and archers are known to keep their distance, taking shots aiming to weigh you down and tire you out so their friends can sweep and impale you.

Directions:
walk to vetallun and stop at a big boulder. Go NE x 3.
Difficulty:
6/10

Creatures:
-various bandits
-archers

Points of interest:
-Bandit Complex
-A large clearing
