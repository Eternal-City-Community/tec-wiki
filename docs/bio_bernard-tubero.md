# Bio Bernard Tubero

**Bernard Tubero - Member of the Council of Elders and leader of the Bright Hope sect**

He holds the current position as **Hand of Ereal** within the [Cult of Ereal](/cult-of-ereal/).

He is the only nominated member of [Tharius](/bio_tharius-allende/). He is the leader of the [Bright Hope](/cult-of-ereal/#BrightHope) sect but is still seen walking Iridine's streets healing the needy for free. A child of the streets himself, he is always mentioned last among the cults leaders. He is fast becoming famous for his healing talents, dwarfing even those of the [High Priest](/cult-of-ereal/#High-Priest).
