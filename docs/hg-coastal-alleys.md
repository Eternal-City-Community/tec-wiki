# Hg Coastal Alleys

### Overview

The coastal alleyways are home to a number of drunken brutes and thugs causing trouble in the middle of the harbor district. They are tougher than their comrades near Septima's.

**Directions:**
walk to toga, w, sw x 2, s x 3, sw, s x 2, sw x 4, w x 2, sw, w x 3, nw, w (one of three entrances)

**Difficulty:**
4

**Creatures:**
-a thug
-a brute
-an alley dog

**Points of interest:**
-Gnaarian's shop
-Polonia's Fish Market
-Egidia's Flophouse
-Door with a carving of a dagger
