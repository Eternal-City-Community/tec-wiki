## Fiona - Vetallun's Resident Healer

A simple healer in the village of [Vetallun](/town-of-vetallun/), Fiona has served to heal wayward travelers and adventurers for quite a long time. A member of the [Morning Light Sect](/cult-of-ereal/#BrightHope) of the [Cult of Ereal](/cult-of-ereal/), she welcomes all injured and sickly into her shrine with open arms.
