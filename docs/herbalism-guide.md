# Herbalism Guide

Below you'll find everything important to know about [Herbalism](/herbalism/).

### In a Nutshell
#### Pros
* **Easy to train** anywhere. You can perform [Plant Identification](/herbalism/#plant-identification-plantid) or [Volume Estimation](/herbalism/#volume-estimation-estimate) in most safe areas with minimal items, even while laying down in your room or the baths.
* **Reputation-based** [lessons](/reputation/#Herb-Lessons) & [items](/reputation/#Herb-stock) available.


#### Cons
* **Many additional tools required** for more **advanced brewing** (mortal & pestal, mixing bowl, fire, mixing stick, etc.)
* **Ingredients go bad over time** and will begin to mold as they near their expiry and disintegrate. Harvesting fresh plants regularly is required.

### Tools
Below are the types of tools possibly needed for brewing. Tools **do not have to be in the character's hands** when brewing *(with the exception of water)* as long as they are in your inventory or in the area with you, they are available for use.

For **recipes that require water**, the easiest way to make it available is to hold an **open container of water in one hand**.

Herbalism **tools**, including water, **are not consumed upon use** the way ingredients are.

#### Brewing - Fundamentals
The cornerstone Herbalism is the ability to make useful products from ingredients, either foraged or purchased.

**Brewing Fundamentals** allows you to create drinks and such.


Ranks in the brewing skills ([Fundamentals](/herbalism/#brewing-fundamentals-brew), [Paint](/herbalism/#brew-paint-na), [Flask](/herbalism/#flask),  [Salve](/herbalism/#brew-salve-na), [Potion](/herbalism/#brew-potion-na)) dictate what types of products you can create.


Characters use the brew <container> command. The **size of the [container](#containers)** determines how much product will be made and the quantity of ingredients needed. For the purposes of the **below chart**, **a mixing bowl** will be assumed.

<u>**Notes on below tables**</u>
* ***Ingredients Description** quantity listed is based on **a mixing bowl**. If using a different sized container, multiply or divide the ingredient quantity using the '*Multiplier*' column in the [containers](#containers) table. If division results in fractions of numbers, round down.
* **Required Tools** refers to items listed in the [Tools](#tools) section.
* 🪙 represents an ingredient you can **purchase** in [shops](/shops/).

### Moves
#### Worthwhile
* **Foraging Skills** ([Basic Herb Foraging](/herbalism/#basic-herb-foraging-find-herbs), [Intermediate Herb Foraging](/herbalism/#intermediate-herb-foraging-na), [Advanced Herb Foraging](/herbalism/#advanced-herb-foraging-na)) - Finding new ingredients for brewing.
* **Brewing Skills** ([Fundamentals](/herbalism/#brewing-fundamentals-brew), [Paint](/herbalism/#brew-paint-na), [Flask](/herbalism/#flask),  [Salve](/herbalism/#brew-salve-na), [Potion](/herbalism/#brew-potion-na)) - More brewing options.


#### Less useful
* [Volume Estimation](/herbalism/#volume-estimation-estimate) - Esimating volumes does not seem to have any purpose beyond informational. Recommended to keep at low ranks and raise only for training purposes.
* [Plant Identification](/herbalism/#plant-identification-plantid) - While identifying plants does continue to provide information on newly foraged plants until rank 100, not being able to identify a plant does not prevent you from creating all the recipes or any other herbalism function.
