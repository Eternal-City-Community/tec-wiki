# Scroll How Do I Name Stupid

How do I name Stupid
   By  Shade -- 5th day Festival of Morning 221
How do I name stupid?
Let me count the ways
From people from far lands
Who do not know 
they live here on sufference
And have scant protection under law.
To petty thieves 
Who steal from established citizens
Never knowing whom else 
their victims might know
And what favors can be called in 
with no notice.
Let us not forget
Those who suffer from vainglory
Arriving barely clad
And screaming of their prowess 
to all the world
When they have yet to learn 
How to get their weapons from their sack
How do I name Stupid?
I could count the ways
I could name names
I could do many things 
but most of those would be unkind.
Incribed to my dear friend Angar, by my own hand, Shade
