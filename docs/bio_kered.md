# Bio Kered

**First Speaker of the Traevant, Champion of Helia, Father to Quail. Defender of the Republic.**

The Traevant Bear, long time spokesperson for the betterment of [Vet](/town-of-vetallun/). Kered is well known to all, he most likely saved you at least once or twice. Warden of the woods, Kered has brought his Traevant to battle against the many clans of the bandit plague. 
Kered holds grudges, avoids the city due to long standing constable abusing laws, his view of right and wrong, has always been simple, defend the weak, destroy the abusers.

Kered doesn't understand why people continue to litter his woods.

Kered for years hated the [Legio](/legio/), with the current leadership, he holds Aurik in high regard and has worked many times to defend [Monlon](/city-of-monlon/).

Kered did not sink [Fran](/town-of-franlius/). That was a lie.

Savior of the [Allendes](/political-factions/#Allende), Kered has saved several of them on the road from bandits, they seem to like to get impaled by spears.

Long live the trout, destroy the salmon heretics.

"Redeemer"
