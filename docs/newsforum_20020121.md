Tuesday, January 22, 8:00pm EST
"Artisan's Guild of Iridine's First Informational Meeting!"
The Artisan's Guild of Iridine will be holding an informational meeting for
all interested artisans. If you feel you are a singer, dancer, actor,
writer, poet, massage artist, painter, or anything at all in the area of
entertaining, performing, etc., you're more than welcome to attend. The
guild will offer many benefits to its members, such as an amphitheatre that
is being built by the guild's patron, Lady Saliea Illandria. Meet at the
Toga. Refreshments will be provided. 
[Also, all members of the Guild of the Arts are to attend, since the two
guilds recently merged.]
Tuesday, January 22, 9:30pm EST
"Skye and Rajean Clean Houses"
Skye has decided to have an auction, and Rajean has decided to join in the
fun. She has given Skye a number of things to auction off. There will be
jewelry of every shape and kind.  Yes, ladies, there is an unique piece of
jade to tempt you, as well as a few other items.  Something for everyone! 
And starting prices will be low!  Come see what you can pick up and
hopefully have some fun. Please arrive promptly at Seneda's auction house.
Friday, January 25, 9:00pm EST
"Legio Muster"
New soldiers have entered our ranks, and it is time to see if they can
fullfill their duties. All Legionaries from both squads assemble outside
the Vetallun fort. Officers arrive atleast one hour early.
[Open for Legionaries of Legio I.]
Friday, January 25, 9:00pm EST
"Let the Seller Beware"
It seems Timmis isn't the only one having troubles these days. What happens
when a lively shop closes suddenly, under ominous circumstances? What could
make a popular and wealthy merchant vanish overnight? Looking for answers
may help find the merchant. 
[Meet at Toga.]
Saturday, January 26, 3:30pm EST
"Now is the Time."
Recruits from the first class of recruits should meet at the crossroads. 
Graduation is finally here! Who will pass, and who will stay a recruit?
[Contact Kered if you cannot attend.]
Sunday, January 27, 3:30pm EST
"Test of Skills"
The second Class of recruits are to meet at Vet. Crossroads for a  small
training exercise. Since this will be the first time out, a trip to Grey
Sands is planned.  We hope to lower the the population of scum that still
controls it with an Iron Fist!
[Contact Kered Solace if you are unable to attend, or contact Phobos with
any questions.]
Sunday, January 27, 9:30pm EST
"In the Round"
The mortar is dry; the last stone has been leveled. The gate opens on the
inverted ziggurat steps of the citys largest stage. Stop by for an open
house, and a short traditional performance. Ask one of the groundskeepers
in the Gardens for directions.
