# Lighthouse

Operating on the **banks of [Signal Tower Island](/signal-tower-island/)**, the **Lighthouse shines from the mouth of the Harbor of the Moons**, guiding ships toward safety in the docks to its south. 

Essentially a constantly stoked fire surrounded by mirrors that direct the light towards the sea, the lighthouse has saved many a sailors life as the trading ships of the Republic navigated the rocky harbor. On Signaltower Island itself crawl many vermin; fluvitur pups, seagulls and crabs run amok. The hunting is pretty good, especially if you dont mind the flavor of roast fluvitur kidney.
