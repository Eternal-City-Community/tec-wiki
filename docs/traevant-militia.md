# Traevant Militia

## The Traevant Militia

The Traevant Militia (TM), lead by First Speaker [Kered Solace](/bio_kered/), was created to protect the Traevant woods and the road that goes from Iridine to the village of Vetallun.

One of the largest organizations around, Kered leads his group in defense of the republic against the bandit onslaught of various factions, each faction seeking a different goal.  Wardens of the road, as well as defenders of Vetallun, the TM have successfully defeated various bandit warlords as well as [Cineran](/cinera/) advances. 

The Traevant is known for rebuilding and investing in the [Town of Vetallun](/town-of-vetallun/), as well as building two roads, one to the [Village of Blackvine](/village-of-blackvine/) and one to the [Village of Seld](/village-of-seld/). Lanterns run from the City Proper to Vetallun, from a project funded, and built with resources from, many of the members of the Traevant as well as residents of Iridine.

To start, the TM was funded by the Senate, and has since progressed to Senator Traevant directly. Currently, a grandson of the Traevant is acting as supporting patron of the Traevant *(when he's not currently hitting on bartenders in various bars across the city)*.
