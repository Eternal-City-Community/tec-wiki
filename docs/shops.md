# Shops

There are many shops, bars, armories, and other vendors throughout Midlight, each offering a unique stock of items. This page attempts to chronicle those items for the discerning shopper.

Keep in mind that the prices listed here are only a rough guide. If your character is charismatic or has certain bartering traits, such as Trader's Tongue, you'll probably fetch better prices than those listed here. In addition, certain shopkeepers, such as those in Parissa's Emporium, rotate their stock with unique and unpredictable items. For such shopkeepers, items marked with a ☘ are examples of rotating stock.

You can search by item or shopkeeper name.
