size 100%The **[Town of Franlius](/town-of-franlius/)** is currently **under attack by undead soldiers**, due to an ongoing storyline run by the Game Masters.

### Tier II - Southwest Corner


**<u>Opponents</u>** 
* **Cineran Axeman *([1HA](/one-handed-axes/))*:** Equips either a tin or bronze axe and a wooden shield. These humanoids utilize stepping moves often, and can knock the lesser skilled opponent to the ground with legstrike. When they are near death, they will temporarily assume more defensive stances. Learn feint or watch for the stance change. The ones wielding bronze axes do noticeably more damage than those with tin.

* **Cineran Conscript *([Brawling](/brawling/) / [Pankration](/pankration/))*:** Uses brawling and pankration techniques. These humanoids can be dangerous in a group if they are not quickly dealt with. When surrounded by allies, they will assume aggressive stances more often. Their critical hits can break bones. While they have the potential to dish out more damage, they are also much easier to hit than the other two humanoids in this zone. Consider knocking them out first.

* **Cineran Recruit *([CKF](/cineran-knife-fighting-knives/))*:** Equips a bronze dagger, usually of poor quality. These are not very challenging on their own, but can be a nuisance when they are grouped with others in this section. They fight more aggressively when they have allies, and stance dance when solo. Watch for the disarm and chopping moves aimed high.

<a id="Tier3"></a>
### Tier III - Northeast Corner


**<u>Opponents</u>** 
* **Cineran Spearman *([Spears](/spears/))*:** Equips a bronze-tipped spear. By far, the easiest humanoid in the Northeast section. These can generally be left alone while you deal with foot and auxiliary soldiers. Can be annoying with the use of parting moves and backwards rolling rise. As is true when fighting any spearman, don't get knocked down.

* **Cineran Auxiliary Soldier *([Avros](/avros-one-handed-swords/))*:** Equips a wooden shield and bronze gladius. Easy to fight alone, but will be disruptive if left alone in a group, but not as much as the foot soldiers. Has some knowledge of missile defense, knows custom one-handed sword maneuvers, and Avros. Important: Keep an eye out for missed Avros attacks, which can leave openings and make them easier to take down.

* **Cineran Foot Soldier *([CKF](/cineran-knife-fighting-knives/))*:** Equips a wooden shield and bronze dirk. Easy to fight alone, but can be disruptive if left alone in a group. Has some knowledge of missile defense, and knows some unpleasant CKF maneuvers. These will fight more defensively when near death.
