# Hg Alleys

### Overview

The alleyways just beyond Septima's lead to one of the cities many unused piers. Drunken thugs and brutes stalk the narrow streets, trying to rob or simply beat up someone who stumbles in. They are apt to join with one another, and sometimes bring their malcontent pets with them to aid in a mugging.
