* SP Gain for attacks: (does not apply at min gains)

 * Regular SP Gain (100%): basic attack, slash, upslash, chop
 * Higher SP Gain (~125%): thrust, charge, weapon strike
 * Much Higher SP Gain (140%): roundstrike
