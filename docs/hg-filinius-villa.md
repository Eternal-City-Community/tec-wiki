# Hg Filinius Villa

## Filinius' Villa

Following a tragic masked party, Turrinio Filinius' estate has fallen into disarray. What remained of his staff to oversee the property has been assaulted by a group of nefarious bandits who now claim control over the luxurious villa situated just north of Seld.

[This is a new hunting ground that has been released yesterday. The estate has been divided into sections that are suited for characters between 1000-5000 TCR.]

This hunting area is best suited for **mid-to-high level fighters** and is located near the [Village of Seld](/village-of-seld/).

**Suggested [@mtarg](/macros-and-targeting/)**: **raider|looter|plunderer|marauder**


#### Highlights
* Trapdoors & secret pathways to explore.

### Hunting Grounds

- Zone 1 is low difficulty (~1K TCR). Rogues and ruffians.
- Zone 2 / 3 is medium difficulty (~3K TCR). Looters and raiders.
- Zone 4 is a mix of low-to-high difficulty, including all NPC from this hunting ground with a low spawn rate. (1-5k TCR) Rogues, ruffians, looters, raiders, plunderers and marauders.
- Zone 5 is medium difficulty (~3K TCR). Raiders.
- Zone 6 most-eastern two spots are by far the hardest (~5k TCR), but the spawn is fairly low. Plunderers and marauders.

**<u>Opponents</u>** 
* Rogues
* Ruffians
* Raider
* Looter
* Plunderers
* Marauders


**Loot**: Pouches, armor, bronze weapons.
