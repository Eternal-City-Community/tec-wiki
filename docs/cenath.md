# Cenath

## Cenath
Little is known about Cenath. Mostly rumors from sailors who may have had a little too much too drink. Cenath isn't a country, but rather an assembly of ships and other floating devices congregating to form a floating "island" of sorts that can navigate the open ocean as necessary.
