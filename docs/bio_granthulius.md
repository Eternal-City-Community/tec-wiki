## Granthulius - 2^^nd^^ King of Iridine

**Granthulius: (48-81)**
Granthulius expanded Iridine territory by conquering the kingdom of [Monlon](/monlon/), and razing its capital. Monlon was the religious center of the Aestivans and Cinerans, and is home to the Oracle of Ereal. He was succeeded by [bio:King Vetallun](/bio_king-vetallun/).
