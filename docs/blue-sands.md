# Blue Sands

Arguably the worst place for a person to live in the entire Republic. This town's sole purpose is to make bricks. Lots of bricks. If you like breaking your back to make bricks that build the Republic's finest homes and slums alike, this is the place for you. If that is not enough, the local beer, "bricker's milk", is popular among the residents here and worthy of a try.
