# Falcata Guide

### Falcata Sword Fighting Guide
Everything you need to know about the Falcata Sword Fighting skill set.


### In a Nutshell
#### Pros
* Very versatile skill set, that **draws strengths from all three one-handed sword styles** ([Avros](/avros-one-handed-swords/), [Nelsor](/nelsor-one-handed-swords/) & [Pardelian](/pardelian-one-handed-swords/)).
* Uniquely **incorporates grappling** mechanics ([Mugger Hold](/falcata/#mug)).
* Two **(2) multi-hitters**, [Smash and Slash (double-hitter)](/falcata/#smash) and [Triple Strike (triple-hitter)](/falcata/#tstrike).
* Unique **draw-and-approach-and-attack mechanic** ([Sudden Stab](/falcata/#sustab)).
* Two **(2) approaching attacks** ([Tumble Slash](/falcata/#tslash) & [Guarded Charge](/falcata/#charge)), both allowing you to **approach a new target even if you're already engaged** (i.e. no need for [Melee Advance](/combat-maneuvers/#Advance)).
* **[Guarded Charge](/falcata/#charge) will even knock an attacker away from your opponent** to make room for you.
* Two **(2) defense-penetrating attacks** ([Heavy Slash](/falcata/#hslash), & [Wide Strike](/falcata/#wstrike)).
* Multiple **knockdown options** ([Leg Strike](/falcata/#legstrike) & [Feigning Thrust](/falcata/#fthrust)). Though [Feigning Thrust](/falcata/#fthrust) inflicts no damage and is rise-able.


#### Cons
* Several attacks and all blocks require a free-offhand, forcing the wielder to **choose between a shielded or shield-less** strategy, limiting your available moves in battle.
* Training is increasingly difficult at top-tier due to **limited trainer ranks** (300/175).
* Top-end weapons are slightly harder to acquire, only available via loot drops. **Good falcatas are not sold in shops**.


### Advice & Tips
* **Using a shield is a more balanced approach**, offering more overall defense, but limiting several high-damage attacks. 
* Going **without a shield** (i.e. using Falcata blocks) **is a more offensive approach**, allowing you to quickly finish off lesser-skilled opponents using [Triple Strike](/falcata/#tstrike) or [Mugger Hold](/falcata/#mug).
* While options exist to both use a shield and not use a shield, **many prefer the shield/sword combos**.

### Moves
#### Worthwhile
* [Heavy Slash](/falcata/#hslash) - A defense penetrating maneuver with good damage.
* [Wide Strike](/falcata/#wstrike) -  A defense penetrating maneuver with good damage.
* [Sudden Stab](/falcata/#sustab) - A unique attack that draws a weapon, approaches and attacks all in 1 motion.
* [Cross Strike](/falcata/#cstrike) - Good damage & default-aims high.


#### Less useful
* [Feigning Thrust](/falcata/#fthrust) - Feigning knockdown, but riseable.
