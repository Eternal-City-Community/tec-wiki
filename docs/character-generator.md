# Character Generator

## Creating a Character - Step By Step Guide 


The **character generator** (aka CharGen) is accessible through the **PLAY** menu while in the Welcome Area **(WA)**.

This **step-by-step process** is used to decide everything from your **character's name**, **appearance**, **[skills](/skills/)**, **[stats](/stats/)** and **[traits](/traits/)**.

To **check if a name is available** for use prior to using the character generator, you can use the **@namecheck** command.

If you create a character, but **delete them within the first 24 hours***(TBC)*, you can **reuse a name of a character you've recently deleted**.

If you **need inspiration** for a new character name, you can always use the **@random-name** command to help you out.

---
You arrive at the Character Generator quiet room.

Midlight, the world of The Eternal City, is based on the culture of Republican Rome. We ask that you keep this in mind when creating and naming your character and that you select a name that is appropriate to the setting. Names do not have to be Roman-sounding, but they should fit in with a fantasy setting. Modern names, nonsense names, and names from any modern media (such as TV, movies, or literature) are not acceptable. If you select such a name, you will be asked to change it.

### Step #20: Trait Selection

**Character Trait Selection**

Certain unique traits will mark your character and distinguish him or her from others.  Positive traits indicate innate talents, heightened mental or physical abilities, and other advantages your character may have.  Negative traits indicate flaws or weaknesses in your character, whether emotional, physical, mental, or psychological.

Every trait is marked by a number value.  Advantageous traits have a positive value while weaknesses have negative values.  For every advantage you pick, you must also pick weaknesses to balance out your character. At the end of this process, the combined value of all your traits cannot be above 0.  Any traits you have already picked up due to the nation advantages won't be used in calculating this value.

You may only pick a **maximum** of **five (5) advantageous** traits and **five (5) negative** traits. This includes traits from nation advantages.

There are also neutral traits, qualities of a character which have both positive and negative qualities.  Of these, you may only choose one.

Remember that this is an optional process.  You may choose the maximum number of traits and flaws possible, or you may opt to pick only a few, or none at all.
