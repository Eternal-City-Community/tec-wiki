# Bio King Vetallun

## Vetallun  - 3^^rd^^ King of Iridine
**Vetallun: (81-105)**
Vetallun, the third King of Iridine, was an [Aestivan](/aestivan-league/) who had come to Iridine and made a name for himself and his family. In 105 EK, he was murdered in a short revolution. He was succeeded by [bio:Quintus the Marauder](/bio_quintus-the-marauder/).
