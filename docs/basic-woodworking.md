# Basic Woodworking

The skillset is new and we have yet to add anything! Basic Woodworking is used in conjunction with Basic Forestry which provides the necessary planks to work with. 

## Skill Overview

Currently empty.

## Release Notes
Woodworking notes:

-This skillset is truly massive. So much so that I've had to split it up on
multiple trainers. The basic trainer in Iridine, Madinia, teaches skills to a
modest level and tier 1 and 2 recipes. This is plenty to get you started.

-Valav has made a return. He can be found at night in one of four camps around
the wilderness of Rock Valley. One is located along the riverside south of
town, one at the edge of the hill west of the tower, one on the edge of the
mountain near Stromheim and one along the edge of the mountain north of the
city. Happy hunting!

-I expect Valav to break at some point. This is an advanced NPC. I've tried my
level best to test him and he's actually been running for several days. Please
report weirdness. He works by talking to him and offering him tools. Want to
learn to nail? Hand him a hammer. Want to learn cabinets? Ask him about
cabinets. Lost? Just ask for help. Again, teaching everything at once breaks
the game. He's a bit of a klutz, you've been warned!

-Tyvana will buy completed components such as doors and shelves, as well as
finished furniture. She pays pine rate. This buyer exists to offset training
costs and generate a modest income. If you don't want to lose money, use pine.

-The overall difficulty has been lowered some from beta testing. Quality on
the pieces is evident from inspection and increases with ranks.

-Josephus teaches sheets, comforters, pillows, cushions and tablecloth recipes
now. These are used to make furniture modifying options. Once completed, they
can be put on the furniture and have their names show up using the restyle
command. Rivitian in the lumber supply along Trade Street sells the stuffing.
This is a permanent thing once stuffed, choose wisely.

-Crafted beds require a permanent mattress. This gets rid of the dumb mattress
on a bed stuff that has irritated players for years. A properly-outfitted
crafted bed will have a unique announcement when laying on it. Go nuts
decorating!

-Carving lores, taught in Iridine, show up once applied to furniture in the
prose and can be included in the name via the restyle command.

-Crafted furniture can be arranged. There are two options, using the arrange
<furniture> item or arrange <furniture> to <object> to allow you to arrange
that dresser beneath the window.
