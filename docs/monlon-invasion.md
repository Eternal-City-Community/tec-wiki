# Monlon Invasion

The **[City of Monlon](/city-of-monlon/) is being invaded!**

> A rumor is circulating around:
> The Kelestians have invaded Monlon! Assistant governor Vaestia Elavia Santum has been reported dead, and the governor Cascar Olgulan remains missing. Although Iridine's heroes continue to come to their aid, the people of Monlon live in apprehension and take shelter when the fighting spills into the streets. Many speculate as to the reason for this attack, but for now it remains a mystery.

> 303rd Year of the Republic, 6th day of Jemros:
> The streets of Vetallun were full of commotion for several days as Legio II finished preparations for movement and began marching through the streets headed to the east. Tall standard poles bearing banners were held closely in front of the formations, wagons and carts in the middle closely guarded by the soldiers followed up by rear security and support personnel.

> 305th Year of the Republic, 4th day of The Festival of the Morning:
> Voices of discontent begin to rise inside of the Republic as the news of an entire cohort being annihilated in fierce combat against the Kelestian forces in Astraea cannot be concealed any longer. Despite the somber news comes a ray of hope in a handful of Legio survivors on the front line doing incredible feats of unconventional warfare with astonishing bravery.

The [Kelestians](/kelestia/) have begun their attack on [Monlon](/city-of-monlon/) and the city is no longer safe. These deadly barbarians now roam freely outside of the city walls, both day and night, while scouts and raiders manage to make their way within the city walls during night. It is **recommended to travel in groups** when venturing outside the city gates.

These barbarians are carrying weapons and armor that had not yet been seen in the Republic before! They make use of [Falcata](/falcata/), [Falx](/falx/), [Chainblade](/chainblade/) and [Slings](/sling/). All four of the skillsets are now available to be learned and the trainer can be found within the Kelestian military outpost in the ravines (not to be confused with the fort near Monlon with the prisoners).

### Enemy Location

* During the night, **Scouts** and **Raiders** make their way within the city walls and will attack anyone venturing on the main roads. These enemies are fairly unskilled. (~300 TCR should be fine)

* Directly outside of the city gates you will find **Mercenaries**, **Warriors** and **Raiders**. (~800-1000 TCR should be fine)

* On the way to the Ravines are the [hg:Monlon Battlefields](/hg_monlon-battlefields/), which is comprised of **Ravagers**, **Mountaineers**, **Menaces**, **Contemplatives**, **Raiders**, **Ascetics**, **Mercenaries** and **Warriors**. (3000+ TCR should be fine with a group - do not venture alone)

* On the way to the Mines you will find **Mountaineers**, **Servitors**, **Mercenaries** and **Warriors**. (2000+ TCR should be fine with a group, 3000+ TCR should be fine solo)

* In the Mines you will find **Servitors** and **Tunnelers**. Along with the regular rats, molerats, serpents and bats.

* On the way to the Barracks you will find **Raiders**, **Mercenaries** and **Warriors**. They are the same as the ones you will find just outside of the gates.

* In the tunnels leading north toward the Kelestian Outpost, you will find **Naturalists** and **Falconers**.

* On the rowboats across Lake Monlon you will find **Divers**.


* Inside the Barracks you will find the regular Chauliodus Sloani, Statorus and Pungopiscor. You will also find Scouts and Raiders, which are the same as the ones found in the city during the night.

#### Armor:

- **Katitra**: Available in tin, bronze, iron and stone. This piece of armor consists of a wide belt of white leathery material that fastens around the wearer's waist and thighs. It supports interlocking stone plates that shift with the wearer's movement. It covers the waist, right thigh, and left thigh. It offers excellent protection. 19 lbs (stone)

- **Uraschada**: Available in stone. The Kelestian uraschada is a knee-length tunic with short sleeves tightly woven from layers of tough hemp fibers. It is made to be breathable, flexible, and to provide the wearer some protection in light combat. It covers the chest, back, left thigh, right thigh, waist, left shoulder, and right shoulder. It offers good protection. 8 lbs 

- **Nagoda**:  Available in tin, bronze, iron and stone. This piece of chest armor consists of a harness and straps of pale white leathery material supporting interlocking stone plates that flare out over the chest, back, and shoulders. Spaced widely for the wearer's movement, a set of vertical plates surround the neck for added protection. It covers the neck, chest, back, right shoulder, and left shoulder. It offers excellent protection. 24 lbs (stone)

- **Manksana**: Available in tin, bronze, iron and stone. This set of armor is designed for wear on each leg and consists of two curving stone plates that meet at the wearer's shin, held together by a white leathery harness. On each side, a third triangular plate covers the top of the wearer's foot. It covers the right shin, left shin, right foot, and left foot. It offers excellent protection. 27lbs (stone)
