# Census And Citizenship

**I. Census and Citizenship** 
 
**Census** 
A census shall be conducted every four years by the Censors. The census will
 account for all Iridine citizens and establish their economic class, based on
 property value and income. 
 
**The Patrician Class** -- are a class of nobles whose well-established families
 may participate in the ruling body of the city-state. In order to qualify for
 senatorial service, an individual must prove an income of 1 million denar per
 year. This income must be based solely on revenue from property holdings and
 estate earnings. 
 
**The First Class (Ordo Equestrian)** --must have property or assets that provide
 income of more than 400 thousand denar per year. This income can derive from
 any source, including trade and commerce. The top 500 Equestrians of the city
 will be given horses for military use by the city -- the &quot;Public
 Horse&quot;. 
 
**The Second Class**--has to prove an income of 300-400 thousand denar per year. 
 
**The Third Class**--has to prove an income of 200-300 thousand denar per year. 
 
**The Fourth Class**--has to prove an income of 100-200 thousand denar per year. 
 
**The Fifth Class**--has to prove an income of 45-100 thousand denar per year. 
 
Those falling under 45 thousand denar per year are the 'Capite Censum', or the
 Head Count. These are not responsible for serving in the military nor part of
 any assembly of the people. 
 
**Citizenship** 
Citizenship may be granted by a magistrate with Imperium. Citizenship is
 otherwise only conferred by birth to a citizen.
