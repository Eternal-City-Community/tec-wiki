# Sling

### Skill Overview 
The sling is a weapon skillset used by the [Kelestians](/kelestia/). It is a missile type weapon, requiring ammo to use. It is **now available** for player characters to learn. It is currently taught in the Kelestian military outpost deep in the ravines (not the fort with the prisoners).

There are at the very least three types of **bullets** that can be loaded into a sling: **ceramic**, **lead** and **iron**.

**For guidance on using the skill set, see the [Sling Guide](/sling-guide/)**.

#### Ammunition:

Sling Missile Weapons can use a wide array of ammunition types to hit targets with and their usefulness in battle can be estimated by using the [Sling Ammo Evaluation](#ammo-evaluation) skill.

|  |  | Ammunition: |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type: | Usefulness: | Location: |
| Sen (coin) | It doesn't have the weight to do much damage. | NPC Pouches, Bank |
| Sterce (coin) | It is fairly light, but might be able to do some damage. | NPC Pouches, Bank |
| Denar (coin) | It is sturdy and could do some damage. | NPC Pouches, Bank |
| Gold Cent (coin) | It has a decent weight to it and would be serviceable on the battlefield. | NPC Pouches, Bank |
| Small Stone | It is sturdy and could do some damage. | Outdoor Survival Foraging, [monlon-kelestian-outpost](/monlon-kelestian-outpost/) (Kavacu) |
| Ceramic Bullet | It has a decent weight to it and would be serviceable on the battlefield. | [monlon-kelestian-outpost](/monlon-kelestian-outpost/) (Kavacu) |
| Iron Bullet | It has a decent weight to it and would be serviceable on the battlefield. | [monlon-kelestian-outpost](/monlon-kelestian-outpost/) (Kavacu) |
| Lead Bullet | It has the weight and density to be deadly in combat. | [monlon-kelestian-outpost](/monlon-kelestian-outpost/) (Kavacu) |

The most basic sling maneuver. Can only be used in the same square as the target.

<a id="sling-clobber"></a>

### Sling Clobber  *slingclobber <target>*

This is **melee attack** can only be performed with a **loaded sling**. This attack requires a heavy bullet.

**When you see this in use you see:**

<div class="skill-template">

A lithe Kelestian menace gives their sling a wide spin before bringing the loaded pouch down towards your head, but misses. You sway to one side to avoid a lithe Kelestian menace's attack.

A tall Kelestian menace gives their sling a wide spin before bringing the loaded pouch down on your head! You suffer a small bruise to your head.

</div>

The maximum bonus for a weapon stance is generally achieved at 100 ranks.

**When you see this in use you see:**

<div class="skill-template">

Deftly putting one foot back, a tall Kelestian menace swings his military sling above his head, rotating it quickly into a blur of motion.

</div>


<a id="sling-timing"></a>

### Sling Throw Timing  *syntax*

This skill functions similarly to Archery - Shot Timing. There is a 2% chance per rank to not hit friendly characters. At rank 50, there is still a 5% chance of hitting other players.
