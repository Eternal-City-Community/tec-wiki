# Hg Rock Valley Well

### Overview
Rock Valley Well is an excellent place for those seeking to begin their journey as combatants, killing blow is not required. Puddles of ichor have come to life and are attacking with their psudopods!

#### Creatures
* a puddle of ooze

#### Loot
* a tiny glossy rounded pebble: a gastrolith stone
* a large glassy stone with forked black lines: a tourmalated quartz
* a large glassy yellow-gold stone with forked black lines: a tourmalated topaz
* a tiny pale violet stone with forked black lines: a tourmalated amethyst

#### Loot Buyer
Stones and pebbles can be sold at Larunda's jewelry shop. From the Rock Valley Town square `walk s 9 e 4 s`

#### Directions
When in the Rock Valley Town Square, `climb well` to enter or exit.
