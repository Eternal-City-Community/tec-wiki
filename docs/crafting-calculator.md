# Crafting Calculator

Use this tool to estimate the raw materials and purchase cost for known crafting recipes.

The calculator is designed to support multiple crafting systems over time. **Jewelry Crafting** is available now; additional crafts can be added once their recipe material requirements and prices are confirmed.

<div id="tec-crafting-calculator"></div>

## How the Jewelry Calculation Works

Jewelry metal is purchased in whole slags. Recipes that consume fractional slags leave usable metal behind, so the calculator rounds the purchase requirement up to the next whole slag and shows the expected leftover material.

For example, a recipe that consumes **3 1/3 slags** requires purchasing **4 slags**, leaving **2/3 of a slag** after the item is made.

The prices used here are the confirmed material prices documented in the [Jewelry Guide](/jewelry-guide/#material-costs/).

!!! note
    Only recipes with confirmed metal requirements are included. Missing or unknown crafting requirements are not estimated.
