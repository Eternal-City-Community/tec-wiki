# Containers

Containers - including the legendary "large sack" - hold items. Each container has a unique capacity and sometimes other mechanical features. Worn containers are worn on specific body parts and layer with other clothing or armor.

### Worn containers

| Object | Weight when empty | Worn on | Examples |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| a large sack | 0.3 lbs | right shoulder | a clumsily patched sack |
| a backpack | 0.3 lbs | back | a sturdy burlap backpack with leather straps |
| a quiver | 0.5 lbs | back and right shoulder | a sturdy leather quiver |
| a sling | 0.3 lbs | back and right shoulder | an oiled leather sling |
| a baldric | 0.5 lbs | back | a dark leather baldric |
| a sheath | 0.2 lbs | waist | a supple leather sheath |
| a scabbard |  |  | a leather scabbard |
| a pouch | < 0.1 lbs | waist | a worn leather pouch |
| a neckpouch | 1.8 lbs | chest | a radiant silver fur neckpouch |
