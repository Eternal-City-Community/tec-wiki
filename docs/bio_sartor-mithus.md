**Sartor Mithus - Member of the Council of Elders**

**Member of the [Sect of the Bright Hope](/cult-of-ereal/#BrightHope)** 

He is both respected for his profound beliefs and feared for his quick temper. Perceived as something of an ascetic he leads the ultra-conservative faction of the [cult](/cult-of-ereal/). He opposes all change and often talks about some vague past that was a golden age for Ereal. Seeing as he has so much work within the cult he is seldom seen in public. His followers, on the other hand, tend to be loud.
