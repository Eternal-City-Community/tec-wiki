# Signal Tower Island Guide

### Guide to Signal Tower Island

#### Overview
Signal Tower Island, which is accessible by ferry, is an excellent place for those seeking to begin their journey as combatants. Seagulls, fluvitar pups, and crabs have infested its rocky shores and citizens are encouraged to kill them on sight.

#### Directions
Walk to Stone Toga Inn, n x 2, w x 4, nw, go n (when ferry comes), go n (when ferry arrives to island)

#### Creatures
* an angry gull
* a fluvitur pup
* a saltwater snapper
* a rock crab
* a fluvitur (located in NW corner in shipwreck)
