## Burial Grounds

This hunting area is best suited for **mid level fighters** and is located near the [Town of Rock Valley](/town-of-rock-valley/).

**Suggested [@mtarg](/macros-and-targeting/)**: **carrion|scarab**


#### Highlights
* Up to **3 possible [tears](/contraband/#tears)** available to collect from this area.
* Collected **[stones](/stones-ores/) can be sold to [Larunda](/services/#Gem-Buyer)**.
