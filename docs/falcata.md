# Falcata

### Skill Overview

The falcata has a distinctive curved blade with a single edge that widens towards the point and then curves sharply towards the tip, forming a hook-like shape. This unique shape allowed the falcata to deliver powerful chopping blows that could easily sever limbs or penetrate armor.

The blade of the falcata is typically made of iron, bronze or tin, while the handle is made of wood, bone, or horn, and features a pronounced curve that helps to provide a secure grip. Some examples of the falcata also feature ornate hilt and pommel designs used by high-ranking warriors or nobles.

The falcata is a versatile weapon that could be used for both hacking and thrusting, and is especially effective in close combat. The Falcata is a weapon first introduced by [The Kelestians](/kelestia/).


**For guidance on using the skill set, see the [Falcata Guide](/falcata-guide/)**.
