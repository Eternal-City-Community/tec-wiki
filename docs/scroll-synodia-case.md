# Scroll Synodia Case

Why is Synodia a heretic?
The first problem with Synodia happened during the celebration organized by my clerk Illun for the
release of the first edition of the Ephemeris. It was held at the Stone Toga's Inn. Synodia showed up
and started to critize the Ephemeris with rude and coarse words.
I attended the celebration and I have been shocked by the behavior of that woman. She was
commenting the burning of the orphanage, telling that those orphans deserved what they got, that the
murderer did a good job. When I heard that I asked to leave and she stayed and started to insult my
House and myself.
Few days after, I complained to the Constable Dracuul that confronted her but she refused to submit. I
came to face her and she kept insulting me. I explained her that she was there for a Disturbing the
peace charge, because he had an unproper behavior when she said "Well you should be used to it.".
I replied that certainly not and she added "I dont care if your were Ereal himself standing there". 
Then I told her that she crossed a line that she shouldn't have and she said
"Now your trying to sound like Ereal." I told her that  some people have finished in the cells
of the Soldiers of Ereal for less than that. And she handed me a parchment like a last offense
or challenge. I don't know telling me to give them that and chuckled.
This parchment is a copy of burnt scrolls mentioned in the Legio report, I joined to the case.
She made a comment about the Soldiers or Ereal with those words: They didn't think Id let them burn
my scrolls and I'd not recover at least some of it?. And as a sidenote, while Dracuul was going to press
the charges, she wielded a dull dark green dirk what is a known to be a weapon handled by the
ravanites.
This woman is an heretic. She supports publicly the murderer of the orphans and a patrician of the
Republic. She stole heretic scrolls to make copies and spread them a around the city. This is corrupted
by her heretics views and tries to spread that all around the city.
Those scrolls are the center of an important plot seen that Cineran spies are investigating in our country
to recover those scrolls that are from the Library of Cair Coradon. The Legio is investigating the
situation that could lead to troubles at the border. I would ask from you secrecy about the parchments
during the trial. The wave of heresy in that city has to be stopped and the content of those scrolls not
discussed during the trial.
Scyro Tyanensis.
