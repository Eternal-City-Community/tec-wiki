#### Raw Weapon Damage

In this damage ranking table, 1 is the best/highest raw damage. Some crushing weapons are not yet included here. Reforged weapon variants are indicated with "(reforged)" after their name. Weapons that appear in the same row do the exact same level of raw damage; that is, they have damage tier breakpoints at the same exact roll over success.

This ranking does not factor in critical hit frequency/quality or the difference between piercing & bludgeoning damage. It simply ranks weapons based on the roll-over-success required to achieve the highest damage tier on an unarmored humanoid opponent.
