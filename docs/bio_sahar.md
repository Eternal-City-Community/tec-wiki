# Bio Sahar

> **Migration note:** Wikidot module `Rate` omitted.

[[/>]]

In the afternoon of the 18th day of Palut in the 294th Year of the Republic, Sahar slit her own throat on the steps of Seneda's Bank. She loved the finer things in life, gems stones most of all. Her agile nature ,modest upbringings, and lofty desires led her to an life of an accomplished jewel thief. After being shown stories of the heretical gods, she ventured far abroad in search of more information. Upon her return to the city, she learned of the trial and execution of her teacher. She rushed to visit the old sacred grounds within the city, but was spotted by the followers of Ereal. Knowing that they would employ measures far worse than death, she chose to take her own life and deprive them the pleasure.
