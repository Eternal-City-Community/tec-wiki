# Monlon Volunteer Guard

## The Monlon Volunteer Guard (MVG)

Founded by Captain **Cassie Klark** and her Commanders, [Retalq Blade](/bio_retalq/) and **Pacolypse Klark** after the war with [Cinera](/cinera/) intensified, the Monlon Volunteer Guard has been assisting the Legio I and II to drive the Cinerans away from the [Holy City](/city-of-monlon/).

Other duties include walking the streets looking for law-breakers to assist the [Monlon Vigiles](/monlon-vigiles/) in keeping the city safe. Besides assisting the city with what else it may need, they protect their Governor Flavius at all costs.

The bright purple and gold colors of Monlon fly high atop a flagpole located in their pallisade a short distance from the Monlon Temple.
