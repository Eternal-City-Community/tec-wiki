# Falx

### Skill Overview 

A falx was a type of weapon with a curved blade. It is devastatingly effective against swarms of opponents, with its unique **Area of Effect** (AoE) attacks which can attack multiple opponents in a single attack. The falx is a weapon first introduced by [The Kelestians](/kelestia/).


**For guidance on using the skill set, see the [Falx Guide](/falx-guide/)**.

### Falx Wild Strike  *wstrike &lt;target&gt;* {#wstrike}

This maneuver attempts to hit ALL targets surrounding you.

* This is an **Area of Effect** attack, striking at all approached opponents.
* This attack can be aimed at the following regions: **mid** *(default)*.
* This attack **cannot** be **aimed at** specific **body parts**.
* This attack randomly decides whether it does bruising, slashing, or piercing damage and can vary on each opponent. Leads to some very interesting critical hits.

### Falx Final Slash  *fslash &lt;target&gt;* {#fslash}

This skill acts as a normal slashing attack until the opponent is KO or should be KO. Once they've hit that threshold it turns into a ranged killing blow cleaving them in two. 

* If the target is low in health, this attack has a **chance to kill** them. As explained by Senses, it does not have a specific threshold or HP percentage that causes them to die if conscious, rather sometimes things in the game (lag, traits, etc.) cause an opponent to stay standing when they otherwise should not be. This makes sure they go down.
* This attack can be aimed at the following regions: **high**, **mid** *(default)*, **low**.
* This attack **can** be **aimed at** specific **body parts**.

**When you see this in use you see:**

<div class="skill-template">

You dip the blade of your retalq falx behind you before sliding your rear foot to the side and twisting into a heavy slash at a gladiator!  He suffers a devastating cut the right thigh!
 
You dip the blade of your worn bronze falx behind you before sliding your rear foot to the side and twisting into a heavy slash at a tall Kelestian raider! Your blade cuts cleanly through a tall Kelestian raider, nearly cleaving him in two! His body slowly slides apart as he dies.

</div>
