# Town Of Rock Valley

## Rock Valley
This very small community is built around a disgraced ex-Senator. The locals support their benefactor, who tends to keep to himself. They are a close-knit bunch, and those who leave retain a strong sense of village identity. 

The current governor is [bio:Calastor Triarchus](/bio_calastor-triarchus/).
