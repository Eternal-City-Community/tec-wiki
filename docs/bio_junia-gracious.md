# Bio Junia Gracious

>


## Junia Gracious size 80%*(deceased)*

Once charismatic leader of the [Soldiers of Ereal](/soldiers-of-ereal/) and **Sword of Ereal** within the [Cult of Ereal](/cult-of-ereal/), she fell when assassinated by a Blackroot tribesman. The survival of her sect seemed in doubt until the advent of a man named Ceajon and a second man known as The Prophet. Little can be said of either of them but they have contributed to keeping the memory of Junia alive and the [Soldiers of Ereal](/soldiers-of-ereal/) have started to grow once more.
