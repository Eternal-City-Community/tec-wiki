# Hg Brigand Treehouse

### Overview
The Brigand Treehouse is home to a group of men and women intent on raiding, robbing, and general mischief. They have been deemed beyond redemption and are no longer contributors to society and can be killed and looted by anyone. The Brigands here are supported by an archer high above their compound who will fire on trespassers below. It is advised to keep the tower clear of any bowmen before proceeding to fight elsewhere.

**Directions:**
