# Codex Sagitum Triarii Vol1

You see a simple drawing of an archery target, laden with only three arrows. 
Only one of the arrows seemed to make center. The drawing, although 
graphical, lacks any outside detail other than a target with arrows pierced into 
it. Below the target is raised and embellished text reading "Archers 
Standard".

Sagitum Triarii via Legio

You read this page of the codex, and notice it is an official publication issued 
in addendum to the subject of the codex.
 "Sagitum are the most elite of archer of the weapons class. 
Weapons proficiencies of the Sagitum require completion of the Archery 
Standards. Combined with the weapon proficiencies are the nuances of self-
defense. The Sagitum will have full combat capabilities to engage the enemy 
up close as with the Hastate, or Principe of the Legio. He will be well 
intermingled with his brethren at the front lines, and hard to single out in the 
mass of warriors as battle ensues. Regular soldiers will regard Sagitum class 
archers as equals in most combat situations. These archers are well 
established among the archers class as fighters. There are relatively less 
goals for Sagitum to cover, outside the Archers Standard outside of Combat 
Maneuvers. For such reason, the Goals tie specifically toward the Archers 
Standard, and its completion graduates the archer to the League of Sagitum."
You notice an official addendum written in the codex on this page.
"Sagitum Triarii via Legio
The Legion requires archers of a caliber prior to enlistment. As the Century 
functions as a group, the archer must hone to work with such group.  It is 
unwise for a soldier to work alone in a surge situation, ergo all units within the 
Legion perform a purpose. The following will be guidelines for the Archer 
class Soldier. There are many job designations for such archers. All archers 
will be able to perform the initial rank requirements. "

Basic Training

You read into the text of this page.
"Goal 1.-Grand Mastership of the Missile Weapon-Bows
Understandably, an individual will want many skills and techniques to their 
weapon for use in combat. An archer may find themselves training in multiple 
skills. But to be clear, the basics will aid all collective skill within the set. It is 
imperative that the archer brings their basics up fast, and foremost. This is 
beneficial to all class of archers.
Along-side their training in the basics, they may learn Quick Stringing, and 
Unstringing to help develop their time with the weapon. As a suggestion I 
would recommend off combat time doing such, though it would be wise not to 
perform such in the presence of the general public."
You notice a number 9 at the bottom of the page.

Basic Training (cont'd)

You read the text of the next page in the codex.
"Goal 2.-Basic Shot to Outstanding
For the general purpose of landing a significant shot, fifty lessons in the skill 
will suffice.  More in the skill can prove lethal. It is to this end, that only an 
outstanding level is recommended.  Such will be covered in the next goals. At 
this goal level, an archer may be able to survive on the ability of his weapon 
alone."
You notice a number 10 at the bottom of the page.

Basic Training (cont'd)

You read the text of this page in the codex.
"Goal 3.-Shot Timing and Aim to Grand Mastership
Aim is the ability to his specific points of the target. The better an archer is 
with this skill, the better with an archers ability to shoot a target high, low, or 
at its middle. This has proven useful at shot rotation, and hitting target 
openings. Shot Timing is the ability to hit a target while it is otherwise 
engaged with compatriot combatants. Such skill is necessary for the politics 
of combat turning in the archers favor. Both skills work collectively to keep an 
archers flights from straying from the target. Only to a Grand Mastership will 
the compatriot warriors be confident in the archers ability. It may be 
requested that an archer demonstrate this ability to prove worth for the partys 
cause."
You notice a number 11 at the bottom of the page.

---
Basic Training (cont'd)

You read the text of this next page of the codex.
"Goal 4.-Long Range Targeting
A competent archer will take this to a Grand Mastership. Outside knowledge 
of this skill, the archer is compelled to fight at a range easily approached by 
the enemy. Though by now the archer should know that toe-to-toe fighting is 
impossible, the ability of the enemy to approach the archer is great without 
knowledge of this skill. Depending on the archers inherent abilities, the range 
of an average archer is between 25-200 feet. Grand Mastership of Long-
Range Targeting raises this ability to 25-1000 feet or more.  Range is ultimate 
protection for the archer, as he can lower his defense to fight without penalty 
of injury to the archers self.
By these four goals the archer may be considered eligible for service within 
the Republics Legion. It is not entirely necessary for the archer to complete 
Goal 4 before recruitment, though it is looked upon with favor. The next few 
months are to be considered training protocol for the Legion, though an 
archer may have already accomplished these goals by such time as 
enlistment."
You notice a number 12 at the bottom of the page. The layout of the codex 
seems to imply an end of a chapter.

Recruit's Regimen

You start to read this page, and notice it implies the beginning of another 
chapter in the Archers Standard.
"Goal 5.-Foot Shot
The Legion requires archers to be able to pin enemy combatants with clarity 
to aid compatriots on front line offenses. This ability is as stated, an aimed 
shot to the foot. Training in the technique shows the archer how to provide 
power in the pull of the string, and penetrate the arrowhead through the foot 
to ground. The combatant should find themselves to a knee, unable to 
retaliate, in preferred scenarios.
Even at a Grand Mastership, all shots may not prove true to the techniques 
intent. However the skill provides the archer such ability. It is preferred that 
the Legion trains the archer in such skill, as completion of recruitment 
harbors the necessity of the archer to demonstrate such."
You notice a number 13 at the bottom of the page.

Recruit's Regimen (cont'd)

You read the text of this page.
"Goal 6.-Head Shot
Another of an archers refined skills; the headshot is a dedicated shot high to 
the cranium. The shot delivers deliberate damage to the skull, face, or head 
area in general. Power of penetration is held back only to provide a blunting 
shot to the area. The purpose of the shot is to stunt the enemy combatant to 
the end of inability to defend, or attack. Such shot is useful for the line fighter 
to deliver their next attack free from defense or retaliatory offense. If the 
archer has proven competent in Foot Shot prior to or during recruitment, 
dedication to this technique will ensue."
You notice a number 14 at the bottom of the page

Recruit's Regimen (cont'd)

You read the content of this page.
"Goal 7.-Rapid Fire
This ability is to dedicate a second shot to the enemy before or during 
counter-measures is taken from the enemy. The skill uses the basic shot 
ability of the archer for the first shot. The second shot is developed by the 
training of the Rapid Fire technique. An equal Rapid Fire ability to the Basic 
shot ability offers no penalty to the second shot. This is under the assumption 
that the Basic Shot technique has been fully Grand Mastered.
It is imperative that the archer has Grand Mastered the techniques of Shot 
Timing and Aim, due to the increased chance of straying flights. Such shot is 
as well, most dangerous, as the archer will concentrate on delivering a 
damaging barrage of flights to the enemys area.
These three goals are the Legions initial proof of worth for their archers. After 
such, the archer will train their defenses to an outstanding level. The next 
level of accomplishments will require that the archer can hold their own in 
tight skirmishes. The Legion does not dedicate their combat effectiveness to 
open range alone. These next goals are to be assumed after and during the 
archers defensive competence engagement."
You notice a number 15 at the bottom of the page. The format of the page 
seems to suggest the end of another chapter.

Sagitum's Path

You read the content of the page. It is the beginning of the final chapter in the 
Archers Standard.
"Goal 8.-Point-Blank Shot to Grand Mastership
This is probably the most difficult technique for the archer to learn.  It must be 
understood that only a Grand Mastership of this skill will prove to be useful for 
the archer. If the archer dedicates his training to singular techniques at a time 
as prescribed by the Legion Triarii, this goal can be accomplished within no 
more than a semester.
It is important that the archer has at least an outstanding lore of Combat 
Awareness with various defensive skills to such level. Training of Point-Blank 
Shot is not recommended until such competence is achieved. This skill 
permits the archer to fight in tight skirmishes, toe-to-toe, and alone in some 
instances. Completion of this skill and ability of defense is desirable for 
Legion purposes."
You notice a number 16 at the bottom of the page.

Sagitum's Path (cont'd)

You read the text written on this page
"Goal 9.-Shot Breathing and Steady Shot
Shot breathing assist the archer to pace himself for firing continuously. It 
works with Shot timing to deter from straying; and works with Point Blank to 
keep from stumbling in toe to toe fights. Steady shot improves the archers 
accuracy. It ties in with Aim to such regards. 
By this level of training, the archer should be in harmony with his weapon. 
There should be a prevalent understanding at what training affects the overall 
shot. Speed, Accuracy, Power, Types of Damage, Training Management, and 
Combat Method will become well understood by the student archer."
You notice a number 17 at the bottom of the page.

---
Sagitum's Path (cont'd)

You read the text on this page.
"Goal 10.-Wind-Shot and Slope Shot
These shots are as suggested by their name. It is not an actual shot, but 
rather the ability to shoot in such situations. Wind is a factor to slow the flight, 
or stray the aim. This offers a reduction in power behind the shot, or 
increases the chance of the flight straying into compatriots during occasional 
gust of wind. Without knowledge of the Wind Shot skill, and archer might find 
themselves seeking a position to fire with the wind to effectively fire at their 
target. Slope shot is the ability to fire on terrain where the enemy may be 
higher or lower than level with the elevation of the archer. Such skill aids the 
archer to fire from elevated position, or at aerial targets. An archer might 
besmirch such skills as petty, or irrelevant, however they have proven their 
worth."
You notice a number 18 at the bottom of the page. You notice by the format 
of the chapters before that this is the end of the chapter.
