# Arandes Pardelian

**Arandes Pardelian**
As a young military commander, Arandes Pardelian defended the Tip district of the City of Iridine after the barbarians attacked the city. For forty-seven days and nights, the barbarian hordes camped around the Tip, occupying Transinvexium and the northern grasslands. The attacks were continuous and brutal, and he was almost convinced to surrender by the members of the Senate. But then, finally, [Adrian Lantos](/adrian-lantos/) arrived to drive back the barbarians.
