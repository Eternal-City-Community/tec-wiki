# Village Of Stromheim

## Stromheim 
The first mountain to the northeast of Rock Valley is [Aziri](/rock-valley-region/#Aziri) territory. The second mountain to the northeast is where **Stromheim** is located, **home to the [Nehal](/rock-valley-region/#Nehal)** tribe.

This is a very simple village, with people of few professions. There is someone who is willing to teach how to use the barbaric [two-handed axes](/two-handed-axes/), but also some tricks on how to move properly. A smith is willing to [Rune](/reputation/#Runing) your weapon or shields, to be better suited for combat. A taxidermist is also willing to work for you, at a small price.

The quickest way to get there, and least dangerous, is to pass by the Tower of Heavens to the east of Rock Valley and from there you will have to head some more to the east, but the village will then be straight to the northeast. You will be able to see smoke and palisades.

The currency used by these barbarians is called [tokens](/wealth/#Nehal). They feature nails, hair, fingers, as well as other things inside amber stones. If you wish to earn some tokens, you will have to trade for them. A trader can be found in Stromheim. When learning from trainers in this village, you can determine the cost of training using the [training cost calculator](/training-cost-calculator/) and the tokens section.

These barbarians are friendly, but they do not talk our language or tolerate violence within their village. Harming a barbarian will result in your expulsion from the village. Please respect these individuals and **do not harm them**.

### Points of Interest
* **[Tokens](/wealth/#Nehal)** are the local **currency** of this village. 
* **Directions to Stromheim** from [Town of Rock Valley](/town-of-rock-valley/) *(outside east gate)*: walk ne 19 e 98 ne 12 *(to return: walk sw 12 w 98 sw 19 )*


#### Entering
The guard at the entrance will allow you to enter if you **offer him [a tear](/contraband/#Tears)** or **say a specific phrase** to him in [Spoken Blackroot](/languages/#Blackroot) asking him to allow you to enter. If you've already been in the village and **exchanged goods** equal in value to ~1,000 tokens, the Nehal people may respect you enough to enter as well.

<a id="Runes"></a>
#### Runes
**Ghendrahda** can place a rune on an organic (bone, wood, etc.) weapon if you reach a certain [reputation](/reputation/) threshold.

#### Trade
[Tokens](/wealth/#Nehal) are the local currency of this village, the can be earned through trade with **Borkos**. Regular trade with the village will increase your reputation to the point of them allowing you into the village more easily.

Ways of **earning tokens**:
* Trading fish to **Orentho** (recommended). *Note: Fish from Iridine are preferred over local fish from Rock Valley. Salmon are preferred over trout. Turtles are also accepted.*
* Trading clothing of *high-quality fabric* to **Borkos**.
* Trading bronze & iron weapons/armor to **Borkos**. *Note: The more interest he shows in an item, the more tokens he will provide.*
