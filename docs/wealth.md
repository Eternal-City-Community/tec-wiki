# Wealth

## Wealth
Currencies are a necessity for life in Iridine. Weapons, clothing, armor, training. All of these require payment. 

While there are numerous forms of currency throughout Midlight, **the most common** type you will encounter is **[Iridinian](/republic-of-iridine/) currency**.

### Useful Commands

These are useful commands in managing **character wealth and money**.
* weal?th
* conv?ert
* pay <person> <#> <coin type>
* hire <person>
* holdi?ngs
* bank


See the full list of [commands](/commands/).

### Currency


<a id="Iridine"></a>
#### [#](#Iridine)Iridinian Currency
Below is the currency used throughout the [Republic of Iridine](/republic-of-iridine/) and recognized by its banks.

The wealth command works with this currency. 

##### Types of Coin
| Name of Coin | Metal |
| --- | --- |
| Sen | Bronze |
| Sterce | Silver |
| Denar | Silver |
| Cent | Gold |

##### Coin Values
In addition to the value of individual coins, there is the *"Talent"* unit of currency that does not have a coin to represent it.

| Coin | Value in Sen | Value in Sterce | Value in Denar | Value in Cents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sen | 1 | 1/3 | 1/12 | 1/300 |
| Sterce | 3 | 1 | 1/4 | 1/100 |
| Denar | 12 | 4 | 1 | 1/25 |
| Cent | 300 | 100 | 25 | 1 |
| Talent | 18,750 | 6,250 | 1,562.5 | 62.5 |

**Note:** You can **convert currency** on this wiki using the **[money calculator](/money-calculator/)** or by using the **in-game** command: **convert <#> <coin type> to <new coin type>**.


```
> convert 250 denars to cents
250 denars is equal to 10 cents.
```


<a id="tokens"></a><a id="Nehal"></a>
#### [#](#Nehal)Nehal Currency
The [Nehal](/rock-valley-region/#Nehal) people and the inhabitants of the [Village of Stromheim](/village-of-stromheim/) use **tokens** as currency. Iridinian banks don't recognize them as currency. 

The wealth command works with this currency. 


<a id="Cinera"></a>
#### [#](#Cinera)Cineran Currency
Cinera has its own currency. Iridinian banks don't accept/recognize them as currency. The Republic doesn't recognize them. 

The wealth command **does not** work with this currency.

<a id="banking"></a>
### [#](#banking)Banking
Several banking institutions exist in Iridine, such as Seneda's Iridine Bank. Banking institutions that are linked allow transfers of money, between characters or between banking locations, for a fee. Funds may take approximately an hour to appear when transferred between locations.

**Banking Locations Include:**
* [City of Iridine (Seneda's)](/forum/)
* [City of Iridine (Steps)](/the-steps-north/)
* [City of Monlon](/monlon/)
* [Town of Rock Valley](/town-of-rock-valley-map/)
* [Town of Vetallun](/vetallun/)
* [Village of Blackvine](/blackvine/)
* [Village of Seld](/seld/)


While inside a banking institution, these features are available using the bank command.

If you're a member of an [Organization](/orgs/), you may have additional options to **Org bank accounts**.


### Payment
Aside from simply handing someone else a sack of coin, there are several options to pay other characters.

Anywhere in the game world, you can use the pay command to pay someone standing beside you. You will need to have the required funds on you and accessible (not in a closed container, etc).

If you're both inside a bank, the funds do not need to be on you and will be transferred from your account to theirs.
