# Fight It Calculator

#### Fight It!™ Calculator

This calculator is a work in progress. Enter your stats and fill in the appropriate slots to get an estimate of your chances at both fighting and defending against the creatures of Midlight! Some creatures may still be missing values. More creatures will be added as time permits. Both offensive and defensive success will likely differ from the one shown here due to stats not being fully accurate. Some features may be missing information.

Note: only Tridents, Staves, Whips and One-Handed Crushing have stance bonus values right now.
