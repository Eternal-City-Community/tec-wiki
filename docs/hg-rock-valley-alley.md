# Hg Rock Valley Alley

### Overview
The northeastern alley is home to footpads and scrawny rats during the nighttime.

### Difficulty & creatures (difficulty similar to coastal alleys)
-Footpad (cold-eyed footpad, lean footpad, 
-Scrawny rat (scrawny rat with vacant eyes, lean rat with vacant eyes)

### Loot:
-Footpads: tin dagger (extremely poor -> average quality), large sack (might contain a torch), pouch (either a pouch with money OR a stone pouch), cloth boots, tunic
-Scrawny rat: stomach (contains gastrolith stone OR tourmalated quartz), pelt, tail, head
