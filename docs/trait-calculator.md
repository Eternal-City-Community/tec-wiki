# Trait Calculator

Select up to **five positive**, **five negative**, and **one neutral** trait. The final trait balance must be **zero or less**. The calculator uses the published costs and descriptions from the [Traits reference](/traits/) and prevents documented mutually exclusive selections.

<div id="tec-trait-calculator"></div>

!!! note "About stats and bonuses"
    Expand **Character Stats & Bonuses** to choose descriptive attribute ranges or enter exact raw values from 0 to 200. The planner applies documented trait percentages (additively for multiple traits), shows effective attribute ranges and adjusted caps, and calculates offense, defense, hit points, and load using the [Stats](/stats/) and [Traits](/traits/) references. Trait effects without a confirmed numerical stat modifier remain descriptive. Temporary modifiers, stat-skill replacements, and exact in-game rounding are not modeled.
