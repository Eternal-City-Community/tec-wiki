### Overview
Signal Tower Island is accessible by ferry and is an excellent place for those seeking to begin their journey as combatants. Seagulls, fluvitar pups, and crabs have infested the rocky shores here and citizens are encouraged to kill them on sight.

#### Directions
walk to toga, n x 2, w x 4, nw, go n (when ferry comes), go n (when ferry arrives to island)

#### Difficulty
0 - Newbie
