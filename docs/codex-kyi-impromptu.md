# Codex Kyi Impromptu

**************************
 
Dedication
 
A group of friends would gather over a cup of tea
and compose on the spot.  A chance word would
prompt poetry, and with that, fellowship.  This
book is dedicated to one who was taken from us
suddenly, but will always be in our hearts.
 
Tovania, you will not be forgotten.

The Journey
 
Oh not again, the waves increase
Will this sickness ever cease
Three weeks upon the raging sea
A better life awaiting me
 
Running from a tainted past
I'll make it better than the last
Smile once again when I make town
and breathe again...lest I drown
 
I'll learn a trade, earn my way
lots of work, a little play
I don't expect a thing for free
Just hope, new life in the Eternal City

They Danced Some More
(a song)
 
Intro
Here is the story of a tattered old inn
where all had a story, at the bar within
the ale would pour and the music would play
till all their cares would drift away
 
verse
One young man lost his whole life
for he, his gambling meant more than his wife
till all was lost...yes all was lost
 
verse
Another told of his sorry story
being tossed from the ranks of pride and glory
and his face went pale...yes his face went pale
 
chorus
So they danced and they danced
and they danced some more
not a foot was still along the old wooden floor
till the ale set in and they felt no more
 
verse
The bartender listened to all the tales
and continued passing out mead and ales
till all had some...yes all had some
 
verse
Where many gather with a common reason
All have problems and it comes in seasons
but they had each other...yes they had each other

Lousy, Horrible, Filthy Boots
(a song)
 
There once was a man with an ale in his hand
walking home one night from the bar.
He was tossed already, and quite unsteady.
He fell, not getting very far.
Through the air went his mug which soaked a young thug
who glared as he got real loud.
The young thug was swearing at the ale he was wearing,
and a constable broke through the crowd.
 
I'm sorry mister constable, I'm not the one responsible,
it's these lousy, horrible, filthy boots of mine!
 
A woman looking pretty and was laughing kinda giddy,
caught the eye of this boot wearing man.
She bade him come near, and then grabbed his rear,
and he thought, I'll take what I can.
She wanted more, his pants hit the floor,
and he reached up under her gown....
when her husband walks in, and he ran out,
quick, bare-assed through the town.
