# Hoplite Combat

### Skill Overview
Hoplite Combat is a skill originally developed by the [Aestivans](/aestivan-league/).

The Hoplite style of fighting allows a powerful, coordinated assault using a spear while protecting the user behind their shield.

Hoplite Combat **requires** the usage of a **shield** and **spear**. The spear can be either a short spear or standard spear, and any type of shield will work. Note that you may find the `1w?ield` command helpful for explicitly wielding a spear in one hand only. The Hoplite style uses a combination of shield & weapon type in determining how fast and strong attacks ultimately are. Because of this, the type of [shield](/shields-guide/) you use is important, because it can affect both attack speed and damage. A bash and jab with a Kelestian assault shield and tin spear will produce a heavy bruise and a light pierce, for example. A combination of boison spear and Kelestian assault shield will be much slower than a retalq spear and buckler.

While this skill set can be optionally complemented by knowledge of [spears](/spears/) combat, note that the two styles have different stances. Many standard spears maneuvers cannot be used with one hand, and those that can will not benefit from their usual Ebros' Scorpion stance bonus if used while in Hoplite stance.

**For additional guidance on using this skill set, see the [Hoplite Combat Guide](/hoplite-combat-guide/)**.

### Skill Details {#Subskill}

### Hoplite Combat Stance  *hoplite* {#hoplite}

This stance gives a bonus to hoplite attacks as well as shield bashes, and is required to perform some hoplite maneuvers.

The maximum bonus for a weapon stance is generally achieved at 100 ranks.

**When you see this in use you see:**

<div class="skill-template">

You grip your bronze-tipped spear and raise your shield vertically before you.

A gladiator is holding his spear to the side with a shield vertically before him.

</div>

### Hoplite Accuracy  *n/a* {#Accuracy}

With enough training in Accuracy, you are able to raise your **perception** in combat situations. A grandmaster in this skill will be equivalent to low-end great [perception](/stats/#Perception).

*(This is a passive skill that improves your perception, making it easier to hit targets.)*

### Hoplite Grip  *n/a* {#Grip}

With enough training in Grip, you are able to raise your **dexterity** in combat situations. A grandmaster in this skill will be equivalent to low-end great [dexterity](/stats/#Dexterity).

*(This is a passive skill that improves your dexterity, making it significantly easier to hit targets.)*
