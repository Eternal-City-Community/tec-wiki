# Bio Abramus

[[>]]

[[/>]]

I was born in the Republic of Iridine and traveled extensively till I met the most beautiful women I have seen. Her eyes as pools of water, a heart like a dove, and a personality like a breath of fresh air. Her name was [Sarai Agape](/bio_sarai/). I had won her heart and thought I would have eternal happiness, but Alas, it was not to be. On the night I was to propose her parents were attacked and slain by a group of bandits. She then gave her soul to the spirit of grief and broke my heart. Now I have vowed to give my life to wiping out bandits wherever they may be and one day winning back [bio:Sarai](/bio_sarai/)'s heart from the spirit of grief. And so began my bandit chasing to Iridine...
