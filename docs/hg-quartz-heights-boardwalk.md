# Hg Quartz Heights Boardwalk

## Quartz Heights - Boardwalk Crawlspace

### Overview

Located within the wealthy Quartz Heights district of Iridine, the Boardwalk Crawlspace is home to a large number of hostile sandcrabs. This area poses little danger to the novice warrior and can be a good supplemental training area for those that are still training in the Ludus Valerius.

**Directions** - Walk to Emerald Square, walk w 5 sw 6 w 10 s 5 w 11 s 1 se 2 e 1 se 2 w 8 s 1 then down 1 (Near 67 on the map)

**Difficulty** - Low Tier (300+ Combat Ranks)

### Hostiles

**a large sandcrab** - These large soft-shelled crabs will swipe at your with their claws, but are not overly dangerous. Their shells can absorb a minor amount of damage, so tin and wooden weapons may have difficulty piercing their shells.

### Notable Loot

**Skinning** - The sandcrabs can be skinned for their claws (2x), legs (8x), and eyes (2x). However, none of the parts have any real sales value - Caprarius offered 1 sen for each part skinned by a novice skinner.

### Notable Dangers

**None!** - This area has no real danger aside from the crabs themselves.

### Areas of Interest

**None!** - Other than the stores and attractions of the boardwalk above, there is nothing extraordinary about this hunting ground.

### Miscellaneous Tips

**None!**

### Map

The Boardwalk Crawlspace is visible in the southwestern edge of the map and is notated as 'Under the Boardwalk'.
