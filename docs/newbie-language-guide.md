# Newbie Language Guide

## Language Learning

### Languages of Midlight
Midlight is home to sixteen nations, each with their own language to be learned. There are also two dialects that aren't related to a nation per se but to a certain region or culture. Below is a list of languages you can learn in The Eternal City:

* **Aestivan** 
* **Altene**
* **Blackroot** 
* **Cineran** 
* **Gadaene** 
* **Iridinian** 
* **Kelestian** - See Kelestian Trainer.
* **Parcines** 
* **Remath**
* **Safelands** 
* **Sostaera** 
* **Steps Lingo** - Steps Lingo is a vibrant and dynamic dialect, born in the bustling slums of Iridine, that encapsulates the resourcefulness, wit, and gritty realism of its diverse inhabitants, weaving together a rich tapestry of slang and colloquialisms unique to the city's most colorful and resilient community.
* **Tuchean** - Tuchean is rich with nautical jargon and expressions, born from a nation of skilled sailors and daring pirates, capturing the essence of life at sea and the boundless pursuit of adventure and plunder.
* **Windward** - The Windward language has influences from both Tuchean and Iridinian cultures (Windward is an island province of Iridine but they speak their own language.)

### Learning a language
#### Welcome to the Crossroads of Cultures
The Great Library of Iridine stands as a beacon of knowledge and a bridge between cultures. Here, adventurers, priests and scholars alike gather to learn the tongues of Midlight, and store works of literature, art, and music. Your journey to becoming a polyglot starts with a single step: a step towards the library's welcoming doors.

#### Navigating to the Great Library

Venturing into the realm of languages is as simple as using your feet! Begin your linguistic journey with the command **WALK TO LIBRARY**. As you approach, let the majestic architecture of the library inspire you with the rich history of and diversity of Iridine it houses within its walls. All of the languages of Midlight can be learned at the library, free of charge.

#### Preparing for Your Visit

In respect of the library's tradition of scholarship and peace, all weapons must be left at the entrance. Fear not, for the guardians of knowledge - the library guards - will safeguard your armaments. Simply use **OFFER &lt;weapon&gt; to GUARD**, and embark on your intellectual quest unburdened. Upon your return, a polite **SAY TO GUARD Return my things** will reunite you with your belongings, along with a complimentary sack for convenience, should you have multiple items.

#### Inside the Library

Upon entering, the air fills with the scent of vellum, parchment, and the silent whispers of knowledge passed through ages. A guard will ensure you've adhered to the library's protocol by gently ensuring no weapons have been brought inside. Then, you are free to explore.

The grand foyer of the library is where your journey truly begins. Approach the desk as you enter with **APPROACH DESK**, where the attendant awaits. **SAY TO ATTENDANT LEARN LANGUAGES**. He will share a bit about each nation's culture and the tongues they speak, igniting your curiosity and thirst for learning.

#### Choosing Your Path

Express your interest in a specific language, for instance, **SAY TO ATTENDANT LEARN IRIDINIAN** and the attendant will offer a glimpse into the culture and history of your chosen nation. A simple yes or no **(Y/N)** command will seal your commitment to learning. Upon agreement, you'll be guided down the marble halls to a tutor dedicated to your chosen language.

#### Engaging with the Tutor

Once in the presence of your tutor, the language lessons will commence. You'll be greeted in the native tongue, and your task is to mimic - to ECHO the greeting, acknowledging the tutor's gender with **ECHO man or ECHO woman**. These lessons can be completed daily, and are available once every real-life day, resetting at midnight server time. Each successful echo will give you skill points (SP) in the respectively language. Once you've reach 1 full language point you'll gain a rank in the respective language. 

#### Immersing Yourself

Each lesson is a step closer to fluency, and with each word learned, you'll find the world of Midlight opening up in new and exciting ways. Engage with the local inhabitants, and forge deeper connections with the diverse cultures that enrich The Eternal City. Once you've reached 50 lessons in a language, you will be considered 'fluent'. You can use the **SPEAK &lt;language&gt;** command to speak in that language. Note that anyone with at least some knowledge of that language will be able to understand you but only those who are fluent will be able to see your speech in it's entirety. 
#### Leaving early
Should you need to leave the tutor before you finish your lessons **SAY TO &lt;TUTOR&gt; I would like to leave.**

#### Embarking on Your Journey

Now, with the knowledge of how to embark on your linguistic journey, the vast tapestry of languages in The Eternal City awaits. May your journey through the languages of Midlight enrich your adventures and open new pathways to understanding and friendship.

## Languages learned elsewhere
Embark on a journey beyond the walls of the Great Library to discover languages whispered in the winds of distant lands and hidden corners of The Eternal City. Each language offers a unique window into the cultures and peoples that shape the world of Midlight.

### Blackroot: The Language of the Nehal Tribe
#### Where Legends Reside: 
Venture into the rugged mountains of Rock Valley, located on the eastern fringe of Iridine, where the language of Blackroot echoes through the village of Stromheim. The language of the barbarian tribe known as the Nehal holds the key to unlocking trade and communion within their settlement. 

##### Journey to Learning: 
Seek out the Blackroot trainer within the Town of Rock Valley. Your quest to learn this language is not just a pursuit of words, but an entry into the heart of a tribe as resilient as the land itself. For directions, cast your eyes upon the map of [Town of Rock Valley](/town-of-rock-valley-map/). Knock to enter. 

### Kelestian: The language of the Invaders
#### Echoes of War:
After 300 years of peace, the reclusive mountain nation known as Kelestia has invaded the Holy City of Monlon and lay claim to the Monlon Battlefields and Ravines. 
#### A Healer's Wisdom:
Within the palisades, a Kelestian healer offers more than just the salve for your wounds; she offers the gift of language. To learn Kelestian, you must breach the palisades with the aid of a locksmith and bear a wound as testimony to your quest. As she tends to your injuries, the language will seep into your understanding, transcending the usual need for mimicry and repetition. Instead, you will glean the language naturally.

### Steps Lingo: The Dialect of the Streets
#### A Tapestry of Voices:
In the shadowed alleys and dillapitated forums of Steps, the eastern slums of the City of Iridine, lies a language born of necessity and survival. Steps Lingo, a dialect as diverse as the people who speak it, captures the essence of a community forged from every corner of Midlight.

#### Earning Your Words:
To weave the fabric of Steps Lingo into your lexicon, you must first earn the respect of its speakers. Venture into a local bar, run by the enigmatic Metian, and win over the hearts of the locals with gestures of goodwill. In buying them rounds, you do more than quench their thirst—you open the door to a world where language bridges divides and forges alliances.
