# Phoenix Guard

### The Phoenix Guard

The Phoenix Guard (PG) are an elite group of soldiers, hand-selected as the best of the best. They fall under the jurisdiction of the [Senate](/senate/), so they do not answer under the normal chains of command that the [Legion](/legio/) follows, nor are they held responsible to the [Constabulary](/constables/). 

Their commander is [bio:Pandarus](/bio_pandarus/).
